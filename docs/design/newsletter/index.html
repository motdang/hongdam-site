How much does a newsletter writer charge?

Beehiiv's guide to newsletter guest writers says writers usually charge between US$0.07 and US$0.20 a word, depending on experience. Copyblogger lists freelance email writers at US$25–50 for shorter emails and US$150–500 for longer ones. beehiiv.com

How much does it cost to lay out an email newsletter?

One Fastwork seller lists laying out one email from your own text and images at ฿850. A new layout with one designed image is ฿1,200, a campaign email with 3 images and a test send is ฿2,200, and a set of 3 emails is ฿4,500. fastwork.co

What does Gmail ask of newsletter senders?

For senders of more than 5,000 messages a day, Gmail asks for SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the sending domain, one-click unsubscribe with a clear unsubscribe link in the message, and a spam rate below 0.30 percent in Postmaster Tools. support.google.com

Can I email a newsletter to people in Thailand?

Linklaters' summary of Thai law says direct marketing by email is permitted if the recipient can easily opt out or unsubscribe. A person can also withdraw consent at any time. linklaters.com

How does copyright pass to the buyer in Thailand?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and registration is not needed. A transfer of copyright to the buyer must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
