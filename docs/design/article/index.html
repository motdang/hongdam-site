What does Google want from an article?

Google's page on helpful content describes content made primarily for people. It asks whether the content shows first-hand expertise, whether it has easily verified factual errors, and whether readers can tell who created it. developers.google.com

Does naming sources make an article helpful?

Google says that giving credit to other sources does not replace the need for original effort. It asks whether content that draws on other sources adds value beyond copying or rewriting them. developers.google.com

How does copyright pass to the buyer in Thailand?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and registration is not needed. A transfer of copyright to the buyer must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
