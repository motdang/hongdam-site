What people ask

What does an award entry writer do?

One UK writer lists background research, a one-hour discovery call, award entry copywriting, two rounds of edits, a follow-up call as required, proofreading and submission of the entry. azendia.com

What does it cost to enter a cyber-security award?

The SC Awards FAQ lists US$400 per entry at the early-bird rate and US$625 per entry at the standard rate. The Cybersecurity Excellence Awards send entrants to a separate fees page. scawards.secure-platform.com

What does a cyber-security award entry contain?

The Cybersecurity Excellence Awards ask for a summary of under 300 words, three reasons the entry stands out, up to three images and supporting evidence. Judges assess innovation, impact and leadership. cybersecurity-excellence-awards.com

Can a company in Thailand enter a cyber-security award?

Some awards are open to it. The Cybersecurity Excellence Awards say they are open to cybersecurity organizations and professionals worldwide, with no country limit listed. cybersecurity-excellence-awards.com

How much time does an award writer ask for?

One UK award writer asks for at least four weeks before the closing date, and says most of that time goes to finding and checking evidence. augustawards.com
