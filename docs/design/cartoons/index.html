How much does a comic strip commission cost?

How long does a drawn comic take?

One Fastwork seller lists 3 days for 1 panel, 7 days for 2 to 4 panels and 8 days for 5 to 7 panels. Another lists 7 days for a hand-drawn character and 15 days for an illustration, drawn in Procreate and delivered as digital files. fastwork.co

Do cartoons work in health campaigns?

A 2025 scoping review of 34 randomized trials of comic-style health material reports mixed results. The authors describe promising efficacy for patient engagement and knowledge, and say more effectiveness trials are needed. pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Is commercial use included in a cartoon's price?

On one Fastwork seller's listing, using the work commercially costs extra, and so does an .ai file. The seller delivers digital files drawn by hand in Procreate. fastwork.co

How does copyright pass to the buyer in Thailand?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and registration is not needed. A transfer of copyright to the buyer must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
