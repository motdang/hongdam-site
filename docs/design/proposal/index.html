Useful facts

  • The US Small Business Administration (SBA) describes a traditional business plan in eight sections: executive summary, company description, market analysis, organization and management, service or product line, marketing and sales, funding request, and financial projections. It says lenders and investors commonly request this kind of plan. sba.gov
  • The SBA's funding request section states how much funding the business needs over the next five years and what it is for. The financial projections back the request and aim to convince the reader that the business is stable and will succeed financially. sba.gov
  • The SBA's startup-cost page splits costs into one-time expenses and monthly expenses such as salaries, rent and utilities. It counts at least one year of monthly expenses, with five years ideal, and says investors and lenders compare the costs with projected revenue. sba.gov
  • The Bank of Thailand's page on SME financial products says a long-term business loan is generally judged on the value of the investment, the value of the collateral and the ability to repay from the business plan. bot.or.th
  • The Thailand Board of Investment (BOI) checklist for the Smart visa S (startup) asks for a pitch deck, which it also calls a startup business plan, covering 12 items. The list starts with a 1-page executive summary, runs through the management team, competition, marketing and sales strategy, financial plan and funds required, revenue model and growth strategy, and ends with the benefit to Thailand. smart-visa.boi.go.th
  • Xero's guide calls a business proposal a formal document sent to a prospective client to win a project or make a sale. Its template has 11 sections, from a title page and executive summary to terms and conditions and a signature box. xero.com

What does the BOI Smart visa checklist say about documents not in English?

The BOI checklist for the Smart visa S has the applicant confirm that documents not in English come with a certified translation. The checklist asks for a pitch deck, which it also calls a startup business plan, and lists 12 items for it. smart-visa.boi.go.th

What does a Thai bank ask a company to hand in with a loan application?

One Siam Commercial Bank (SCB) SME loan page lists, for a company: a company certificate from the Department of Business Development with the shareholder list, the memorandum of association, ID copies of the shareholders, directors and guarantors, audited financial statements for 3 years, at least 6 months of main-bank statements, consent for a credit bureau check, and a copy of the collateral title. The page is for firms with yearly sales of ฿75,000,000–500,000,000. scb.co.th
