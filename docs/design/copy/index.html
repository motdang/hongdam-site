What does a UK copywriter charge for a website page?

A 2026 UK price guide lists website page copy at £200–600 a page, and full website copy of 5 to 10 pages at £1,000–5,000. whito.co.uk

Can one site have pages in Thai and English?

Yes. Google asks sites to use hreflang to tell it about the language versions of a page. Each version lists itself and all the others. Google treats translated versions as duplicates only when the main content stays untranslated. developers.google.com

What does Google look for in website text?

Google's page on helpful content asks whether the text is written for people first, whether readers can tell who created it, and whether it shows expertise on the topic. developers.google.com

How does copyright pass to the buyer in Thailand?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and registration is not needed. A transfer of copyright to the buyer must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
