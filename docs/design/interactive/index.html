What does WCAG say about moving content on a page?

Success criterion 2.2.2, Level A, covers moving, blinking or scrolling information that starts automatically, lasts more than five seconds and sits beside other content. It asks for a way for the user to pause, stop or hide it. It leaves out movement that is essential to the activity. w3.org

Who owns the code and art of a game?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and computer programs are listed with literary works. A transfer of copyright must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
