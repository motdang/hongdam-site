What file does a laser cutter need?

A laser-cutting service says it highly recommends SVG and also takes DXF, AI, EPS and PDF. Each style has a colour code, and the service reads the colours set on the paths to suggest the matching style. learn.sculpteo.com

How long does copyright on a pattern last?

In Thailand copyright lasts for the life of an identifiable author plus 50 years. For applied art it lasts 50 years from creation or first publication. Copyright covers a drawn work, not ideas, methods, systems or concepts. tilleke.com

What is a kranok?

Kranok is the flame-shaped ornament in Thai art. Hongdam's page Kranok, Drawn builds the flames from equations: sliders change the curl and the notches, and a visitor can draw a line that curls at its end and grows flames, then save the picture. nanobotco.github.io
