Good to know

  • The Thailand Board of Investment (BOI) lists a pitch deck, which it also calls a startup business plan, among the documents for the Smart visa S (startup). Its sheet names 12 items for the deck, from a 1-page executive summary and the management team to the revenue model, the growth strategy and Thailand's benefit. smart-visa.boi.go.th
  • Y Combinator's seed deck template runs: title, problem, solution, traction, what makes the team special, business model, market, team, and the ask. One slide per topic is ideal in the template, and three is the most it expects. ycombinator.com
  • Sequoia Capital's outline for writing a business plan names 10 sections: company purpose, problem, solution, why now, market potential, competition, business model, team, financials and vision. sequoiacap.com
  • DocSend's data shows investors spend less than 3 minutes reviewing a pitch deck. DocSend tracks how decks are shared and viewed on its platform, and joins that data to founders' fundraising outcomes. dropbox.com
  • In DocSend's 2020 pre-seed data, investors spent over four minutes on decks that raised money and one minute 30 seconds on decks that did not. None of the decks that raised money in its dataset had a table of contents, and they more often put the product and Why now sections early. dropbox.com
  • CBS News describes Guy Kawasaki's 10/20/30 rule: 10 slides, no more than 20 minutes, and text no smaller than 30 points. The reason given for 10 is that people cannot take in more than ten ideas in one sitting. cbsnews.com
  • In Thailand, copyright arises when a work is made; registration is not needed. tilleke.com

What people ask

How many slides does a pitch deck have?

DocSend says most pitch decks are roughly 20 pages long, with about 50 words per slide. It sorts the content into 12 sections in four buckets: an overview, money, product-market fit and the ask. It also says investors typically give a deck a couple of minutes or less. dropbox.com

How long do investors look at a pitch deck?

A TechCrunch report on DocSend's data, dated 22 September 2022, says a founder has just under three minutes on average to earn a meeting. It puts the time investors spent on decks that failed to raise money at 2 minutes 13 seconds. Product and business model were the sections that held investors longest. techcrunch.com

Why does the 10/20/30 rule set a text size?

CBS News calls 30 points the smallest size text should appear anywhere in the deck. Its reason is that smaller text tempts a presenter to put the whole story on the slides and read from them. It adds that a 20-minute pitch in a one-hour slot leaves nearly 40 minutes for questions. cbsnews.com

What goes on the problem and solution slides?

Sequoia's outline asks the problem section to describe the customer's pain, how it is handled today and where current solutions fall short. The solution section explains the main insight behind the product, why its value proposition is distinctive and durable, and where it goes next. sequoiacap.com

Does a seed deck need a fancy design?

Y Combinator calls its seed deck template intentionally simple in design. It tells founders to strive for clarity and concision and to focus on narrative. It points to a piece by Kevin Hale on how to design decks well. ycombinator.com

What does the BOI Smart visa S sheet say about a pitch deck?

The sheet lists a pitch deck as document 5 of its required documents. It names 12 items for the deck, among them the competition, the marketing and sales strategy and the revenue model. The applicant also confirms that documents not in English come with a certified translation. smart-visa.boi.go.th

Who writes the content of a pitch deck?

One Fastwork seller of pitch decks sets the split by package. Small is 100% from the buyer's brief. Medium is 70% brief and 30% team input. Large and XL are 50% brief and 50% or more team input. The process starts with a call about the details and the brief. fastwork.co

Is there a free tool for making slides?

Pitch's free plan costs €0 for 1–5 seats. It includes unlimited presentations, custom templates, basic sharing links, 2 external guests and 100 AI credits that do not renew once used. The Plus plan is €10 a month, billed yearly. pitch.com
