How long can a YouTube Short be?

YouTube's help page says Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long. support.google.com

Can a business use any song in a TikTok video?

TikTok says no. Songs in its General Music Library are not cleared for content from a brand. The Commercial Music Library, reached from a Business Account, holds more than 600,000 tracks cleared for branded content and ads. ads.tiktok.com

How many people in Thailand are on TikTok?

DataReportal puts TikTok's advertising reach in Thailand at 56.6 million adults at the end of 2025, 96.3 percent of those aged 18 and over. It notes that this is an advertising reach estimate, not a count of monthly active users. datareportal.com
