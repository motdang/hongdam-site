What people ask

How much does an illustrated map cost?

One Fastwork seller with 519 orders lists a hand-drawn line-art map at ฿1,900 for up to 15 places, a 3D isometric map at ฿2,700 and a map in both styles at ฿6,500. Changes beyond the 5 included cost ฿200–300 each. fastwork.co

Can a map be drawn from OpenStreetMap data?

OpenStreetMap says its data is open data under the ODbL. Anyone who uses it gives credit by showing its attribution notice and makes clear that the data is under the ODbL. openstreetmap.org

What files does a map artist hand over?

How much does an infographic cost?

One Fastwork seller with 247 orders lists an infographic at ฿1,000, delivered in 1 day in Ai, JPG, PNG and PDF files with 3 changes. Each extra change is ฿100. fastwork.co

How does copyright pass to the buyer in Thailand?

In Thailand copyright arises when a work is made, and registration is not needed. A transfer of copyright to the buyer must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
