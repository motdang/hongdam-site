Useful facts

  • In Thailand, copyright arises when a work is made; registration is not needed. tilleke.com
  • Thai trademarks go to whoever files first, not whoever used the mark first. harris-sliwoski.com
  • A registered Thai trademark lasts 10 years from filing and renews for 10 more. tilleke.com
  • The Department of Intellectual Property's service guide lists a filing fee of ฿1,000 an item, for up to 5 items in a class, and ฿9,000 for a class of more than 5 items. Once the mark is accepted, the fee is ฿600 an item, or ฿5,400 for a class of more than 5 items. webnew.ipthailand.go.th
  • The same guide lists 16 months from filing to certificate: 6 months to examine the mark, 5 for the registrar's decision, 2 to prepare the announcement and a 60-day public announcement. webnew.ipthailand.go.th
  • A Thai print shop's FAQ asks for AI, PDF, PNG or JPG files at high resolution. page.line.me
  • A Fastwork logo listing hands over the source file (.ai), a PNG with a clear background and a JPG. Its packages also list a print file at 300 dpi. fastwork.co

How much does it cost to register a logo as a trademark in Thailand?

The Department of Intellectual Property's service guide lists ฿1,000 an item to file, for up to 5 items in a class, and ฿9,000 for a class of more than 5 items. When the mark is accepted, the fee is ฿600 an item, or ฿5,400 for a class of more than 5 items. webnew.ipthailand.go.th

How long does it take to register a trademark in Thailand?

The Department of Intellectual Property's guide lists 16 months in all. Examining the mark takes 6 months, the registrar's decision 5, preparing the announcement 2, and the public announcement runs 60 days. webnew.ipthailand.go.th
