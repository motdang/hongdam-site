Good to know

  • Under Thai data protection law a request for consent must be made in writing or electronically, and a person can withdraw consent at any time. linklaters.com
  • Thai law defines personal data as data about a natural person that can identify that person, directly or indirectly. linklaters.com
  • The Oral History Association lists getting and documenting the narrator's informed consent as a core step. It advises a signed legal release ideally when the interview is completed, and, when possible, a chance for the narrator to approve the oral history before public release. oralhistory.org
  • Thai copyright in an audiovisual work lasts 50 years from creation or first publication. The moral rights of an author include the right to be identified as the author. tilleke.com
  • A Thai copyright assignment must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com

Is editing included in a shoot price?

On that Fastwork videographer's listing, editing is not included. It is done only as an add-on service when the client asks for it. fastwork.co

Does the person interviewed sign anything?

The Oral History Association's best practices list informed consent from the narrator, a formal record of the agreement before recording starts, and a signed legal release form, ideally when the interview is completed. oralhistory.org

Can a person take back consent after the interview?

Under Thai data protection law a person can withdraw consent at any time, and a request for consent must be made in writing or electronically. Thai law counts data that can identify a person, directly or indirectly, as personal data. linklaters.com

How long does copyright on an interview video last?

In Thailand copyright in an audiovisual work lasts 50 years from creation or first publication. A transfer of copyright must be in writing and name a term. With no term written, it is 10 years. tilleke.com
