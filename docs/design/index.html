Useful facts

  • In Thailand, copyright starts when a work is made. You do not need to register it. tilleke.com
  • A Thai trademark (a registered brand name or logo) goes to whoever applies first, not to whoever used it first. harris-sliwoski.com
  • A registered Thai trademark lasts 10 years from the day you apply and can be renewed for 10 more years. tilleke.com
