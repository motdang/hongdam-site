What people ask

What does “This site may be hacked” mean?

Google adds it to a result when it believes a hacker changed pages on the site or added spam pages. Google says a visit could lead to spam or malware. support.google.com

How does the label come off?

Google says it stays until the site owner acts. Once the whole site is clean, the owner asks Google to check it from the Security Issues section of Search Console. After Google checks the fix, it removes the label. support.google.com

How long does Google take to review a cleaned site?

A few days for a malware infection, and up to several weeks for a site hacked with spam. When the site passes, browser and search flags go away within 72 hours. web.dev

Does malware removal cost extra?
