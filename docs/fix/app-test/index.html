Useful facts

  • Google Play asks a personal developer account created after November 13, 2023 to run a closed test before it applies for production access. The test needs at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row. support.google.com
  • Google Play's pre-launch report installs the app on test devices and reports stability, Android compatibility, performance and accessibility issues. The devices run Android 9 and above and are located in the United States. support.google.com
  • The pre-launch crawler taps, types and swipes for several minutes. Results usually come within an hour of upload, and sometimes take several hours. support.google.com
  • Apple's TestFlight takes up to 10,000 external testers and up to 100 internal testers, who are members of the developer's team. developer.apple.com
  • A TestFlight build can be tested for up to 90 days. For external testers, the first build of an app in a group goes to App Review first. developer.apple.com

What people ask

How many testers does Google Play ask for before release?

For a personal developer account created after November 13, 2023, at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 days in a row, in a closed test. support.google.com

What does Google's pre-launch report test?

It lists stability, Android compatibility, performance and accessibility issues, found on test devices that run Android 9 and above. The devices are located in the United States. support.google.com

How long does the pre-launch report take?

Google says results usually come within an hour of uploading the app bundle. In some cases they take several hours. support.google.com

How many people can test an iPhone app before release?

TestFlight takes up to 10,000 external testers and up to 100 internal testers. The page also says testers can use available builds on up to 30 devices. developer.apple.com
