What size should Facebook post pictures be?

A Wongnai page dated 20 May 2020 lists horizontal 1200 × 800 pixels (4:3), square 1040 × 1040 and vertical 960 × 1200 (4:5) for a Facebook post, and 800 × 800 or 1040 × 1040 for photos inside an album. wongnai.com

How many people in Thailand use Facebook?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Thailand gives Facebook an ad reach of 51.5 million people in late 2025, 71.9% of the population. It says reach figures are advertising estimates and may not match active user counts. datareportal.com

Does a page that sells need to be registered?
