What people ask

Does a market stall need a price sign?

In February 2024 the Department of Internal Trade told vendors to post clear signs with the quantity and price of products. It named street-market food stalls and made-to-order restaurants as the main targets. mgronline.com

How can a customer pay a stall by QR code?

With Thai QR Payment, the customer scans the stall's QR code in a mobile banking app, and the money moves by PromptPay. The Bank of Thailand lists transfers up to ฿5,000 as free of fee. bot.or.th

How is a fake payment slip spotted?

SCB lists letters or numbers in different fonts or thickness, a sender name or time that does not match, and a QR code that cannot be verified. Its SCB EASY app scans a slip's QR code. scb.co.th

When do the Chiang Mai walking streets run?

The municipality's page lists the Sunday market and the Saturday market on Wua Lai Road, each from about 17:00 to 22:00. Its history section says the Sunday hours changed to 15:00–22:00. cmcity.go.th
