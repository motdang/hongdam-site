Useful facts

  • For a Thai sole proprietor, Grab's merchant page lists a copy of the ID card (the applicant is 20 or older and the card is not expired) and a copy of a bank statement or passbook from Kasikorn, Krungthai or Krungsri. The account is a savings or a current account. grab.com
  • For a foreign owner, the same page lists a passport that is not expired, a work permit, a foreign business licence (FBL), proof of a Thai address such as a lease or bills, and the owner's bank account. grab.com
  • Grab's merchant page says the approval email with the e-contract comes in 3–5 working days after a website application. Its steps for an app application say 1–3 working days. After the contract is signed, the shop adds its menu, photos and details in the GrabMerchant app and unpauses the store. grab.com
  • ShopeeFood's help centre says a shop applies in the Shopee Partner app, fills in its details, attaches documents and is verified with an ID card and a bank account. The system reviews and approves within 7–10 working days after the form is sent. help.shopee.co.th
  • Wongnai's page on joining LINE MAN GP through the Wongnai Merchant App, dated 27 August 2021, lists a copy of the ID card (age 20 or older) and a copy of a savings account book for a sole proprietor. A company adds a VAT registration copy (ภ.พ. 20) and a company certificate, and the account is in the company's name. wongnai.com

What people ask

What does a restaurant need to sign up for GrabFood?

A Thai sole proprietor brings a copy of the ID card and a copy of a bank statement or passbook from Kasikorn, Krungthai or Krungsri. A Thai company brings its registration certificate, VAT certificate (PP20), shareholders' list, a committee member's ID card and a company bank account copy. Both lists are on Grab's merchant page. grab.com

How much does GrabFood charge a restaurant?

Grab's merchant blog, posted 9 August 2024, says there is no entry or sign-up fee, and that Grab keeps up to 30% of the order value. The post also mentions a monthly service fee as a share of sales and gives no rate for it. grab.com

How long does Grab take to approve a shop?

Grab's merchant page gives 3–5 working days for the approval email after a website application, and 1–3 working days in the steps for an app application. After the contract is signed, the GrabMerchant app guide arrives within 2 hours. grab.com

Can a foreigner sign up a shop on Grab?

Grab's merchant page lists papers for foreigners: a passport, a work permit, a foreign business licence (FBL), proof of a Thai address and the owner's bank account. A foreign-owned company brings the same company papers as a Thai one, plus the licence and the committee members' passports. grab.com

How does a shop join LINE MAN?

Wongnai's 2021 guide describes the steps: open the Wongnai Merchant App, tap the GP Partnership banner on the Home tab, press สมัคร LINE MAN GP and upload the papers. The papers are signed by hand. The guide gives no approval time or GP rate, and the page is dated 27 August 2021. wongnai.com
