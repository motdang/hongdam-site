Good to know

  • In a post dated 27 October 2025, Meta says advertisers whose ads reach Thailand and who meet its criteria must verify who benefits from and pays for their ads. The names show in the ad's Ad info and in the Ad Library. A notified advertiser has up to 7 days to finish, or its ads are not delivered to users in Thailand. developers.secure.facebook.com
  • TikTok's budget page says a campaign's daily or lifetime budget must exceed US$50 and an ad group's daily budget must exceed US$20. An ad group's lifetime budget is US$20 times the days it runs. ads.tiktok.com
  • Google Ads treats the daily budget as an average. On any day it can charge up to two times the average daily budget for most campaigns, and in a month up to 30.4 times. A US$10 budget allows US$20 on a day and US$304 in a month. support.google.com
  • In late 2025 Facebook's ad reach in Thailand was 51.5 million people (71.9% of the population), TikTok's 56.6 million adults and Instagram's 20.6 million. DataReportal says these are advertising reach estimates, which may not match active user counts. datareportal.com

What people ask

How much does it cost to have someone run Facebook ads?

One Thai agency lists a service fee of ฿4,500 a month and ad spend of ฿15,000, both before 7% VAT, for promoting a post, a page or a website with remarketing. The page says unspent ad money carries over to the next month. smejump.co.th

Is the ad spend part of the agency's fee?

The four package fees on one Thai agency's page leave out the ad spend itself. Its FAQ gives ฿5,000–10,000 a month as a minimum ad spend. For a company, the price is the base price times 1.04 for withholding tax and VAT. myproads.com

What is the minimum budget on TikTok ads?

TikTok's budget page says a campaign's daily budget or lifetime budget must exceed US$50, and an ad group's daily budget must exceed US$20. An ad group's lifetime budget is US$20 times the total days, past days included. ads.tiktok.com

How does a Google Ads daily budget work?

Google says the average daily budget is the planned daily spend, averaged over the month. Spend can run under it on some days and over it on others, up to two times the budget on a day and 30.4 times in a month for most campaigns. Dividing a monthly amount by 30.4 gives the daily figure. support.google.com

Does Meta verify advertisers in Thailand?

Meta's post of 27 October 2025 says advertisers whose ads reach Thailand and who meet its criteria must verify who benefits from and pays for their ads. The names appear in Ad info and the Ad Library. A notified advertiser has up to 7 days, and without verification its ads are not delivered to users in Thailand. developers.secure.facebook.com
