Useful facts

  • Shopee Thailand sets its sale fee by product category. From 4 August 2026 the table for ordinary sellers (Non-Mall) runs from 7.49% for phones and tablets to 17.12% for clothes, bags and shoes, with VAT included. Most categories, among them home, beauty and food, are 16.05%. drive.google.com
  • Lazada Thailand's marketplace service fee, from 1 August 2026 and before VAT, is 5.5% to 15% for electronics, 7.5% to 16% for fashion, 9.5% to 15% for general goods and 15% for consumer goods. The payment fee is 3.21% with VAT. sellercenter.lazada.co.th
  • TikTok Shop Thailand's commission for ordinary sellers (Non-Mall), from 5 July 2026 and with tax included, runs from 6.42% to 11.77% by category. Most categories are 10.70%; clothes, bags and shoes are 11.77%. seller-th.tiktok.com
  • TikTok Shop adds a commerce growth fee on each item: 8.03%, or 6.96% for electronics, tax included, capped at ฿199 an item. It is waived for the first 30 days after a new seller joins, up to ฿1,000 for sellers who join after 16 July 2026. seller-th.tiktok.com
  • PromptPay has run since 2016. Money moves by citizen ID, mobile phone number or bank account number, and a phone number links to one account. Transfers up to ฿5,000 carry no fee; the higher tiers cost up to ฿10. bot.or.th
  • Individuals who sell online register with the Department of Business Development under the Commercial Registration Act. A company registered with the department, holding a commercial registration certificate, needs no separate e-commerce licence. legal500.com
  • The Department of Business Development issues two marks for online sellers. DBD Registered confirms that the person or company exists. DBD Verified goes to registered sellers that meet its criteria and is valid for one year. The Ministry of Commerce invites sole proprietors and companies to apply. moc.go.th

What people ask

How much does it cost to set up an online shop?

One Fastwork freelancer lists three shop-site packages: ฿4,900, ฿9,800 and ฿13,400, delivered in 7, 14 and 21 days. The ฿9,800 package lists a shopping cart, a domain, hosting and up to 10 products. The ฿13,400 package adds a payment notification system, a two-language site and Facebook login. fastwork.co

Can I start an online store for free?

Lazada's seller page lists four charges at 0% for 30 days for new sellers: the payment fee, the marketplace service fee, the special free-shipping programme and the daily cashback programme. The page gives no rates for after the 30 days. pages.lazada.co.th

What does Shopee charge besides the sale fee?

Shopee Thailand's fee policy lists a payment transaction fee of 3% and a platform infrastructure fee of ฿1 for each successful order, both before VAT. The sale fee depends on the category and has its own table. Sellers with ฿10,000 of sales or less in the past 30 days may get a discount of 0.5% to 1.5% on the sale fee. help.shopee.co.th

Does PromptPay cost less than a card?

On Omise's Thailand price list, PromptPay QR costs 1.65% and cards 3.65% of each successful payment, before 7% VAT. Mobile banking and direct debit cost ฿10 a payment. omise.co

What does PromptPay need on the seller's side?

PromptPay links a citizen ID, a mobile phone number or a bank account number to a bank account, and a phone number links to one account only. The Bank of Thailand lists PromptPay for Corporate, which lets a business receive money from customers, and Thai QR Payment, where the buyer scans a code in a banking app. bot.or.th

Does an online shop need to be registered?

Selling online falls under the Commercial Registration Act, so individuals who sell online register with the Department of Business Development. A company registered with the department and holding a commercial registration certificate needs no separate e-commerce licence. legal500.com

Does an online shop pay VAT?

The Revenue Department's VAT page says a person who regularly supplies goods in Thailand with yearly turnover above ฿1,800,000 is subject to VAT. Registration is before the business starts or within 30 days after income reaches that figure. The rate on the page is 7%. rd.go.th
