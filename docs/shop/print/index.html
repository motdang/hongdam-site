How much does a QR table sign cost to print?

Gogoprint lists table tents of 23 × 13 cm at ฿2,225 for 100 (฿22.25 each), ฿9,670 for 500 and ฿14,600 for 1,000. gogoprint.co.th

What size is a table tent?

Gogoprint's table tent is 23 × 13 cm across, its popular size, or 13 × 23 cm upright. The paper is 260 or 300 gsm art card. gogoprint.co.th

Does a QR code need a margin around it?

Yes. The website qrcode.com says a margin is required around a QR Code. It does not give a width on that page, and it says the module size depends on the printer and the reading device. qrcode.com

Must a stall show its prices on a sign?

In February 2024 the Department of Internal Trade told vendors to post clear signs with the quantity and price of products, and named street-market food stalls and made-to-order restaurants as the main targets. mgronline.com
