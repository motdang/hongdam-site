What people ask

How much does it cost to translate a menu?

A Fastwork translator who lists hotel, food and drink text offers Thai–English and English–Thai at ฿250 for one package, with 5 days and 1 revision. The listing does not give a word or page count and does not mention menus by name. fastwork.co

Can a phone app translate a menu or sign?

Google Translate's help page for Android says the camera can translate text found in front of it, such as signs or handwritten notes: point it at the text and tap the shutter. The page does not mention menus. It says small, unclear or stylized text may not be translated accurately. support.google.com

Must a menu show prices?

In February 2024 the Department of Internal Trade told food vendors to show the quantity and price of each product on a sign, so a customer sees the price before buying. Its hotline is 1569. mgronline.com

Can a restaurant charge visitors from abroad more?

The Department of Internal Trade's February 2024 notice, as reported by Manager Online, says restaurants may not raise food prices for foreign visitors. The department's hotline for reports is 1569. mgronline.com

How many visitors from abroad come to Thailand?

A release of the Tourism Authority of Thailand dated 16 October 2026 gives 23.4 million international arrivals from 1 January to 30 September 2026 and a forecast of 33 million for the year. tatnews.org
