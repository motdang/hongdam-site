What people ask

How does a restaurant reply to a Google review?

Google's steps: on the Business Profile, select Read reviews, press Reply next to a review, write the text and press Reply. The business has to be verified first. The reply is public, and the reviewer is notified. support.google.com

Can a restaurant delete a bad Google review?

Google's page describes flagging a review that breaks its content policies. It does not say an owner can delete a review. An owner can delete their own reply. support.google.com

How much does Grab take from a restaurant?

Grab's merchant blog, posted 9 August 2024, says Grab keeps up to 30% of the order value and charges no sign-up fee. The post also mentions a monthly service fee as a share of sales, with no rate. grab.com

What does Google Business Profile care cost a month?

One Thai agency lists a bundle from ฿1,900 a month: profile set-up, verification, over 20 photos, about 20 directory listings, a plan for requesting reviews, review replies, weekly Google posts and ranking reports. It lists no binding contract, 7 days' notice to cancel and a 30-day free trial. loyalcloudcrm.com

Must a restaurant put its prices on display?

In February 2024 the Department of Internal Trade told food vendors to show the quantity and price of each product on a sign, so a customer sees the price before buying. The same article gives its hotline, 1569, for reports about overpricing. mgronline.com
