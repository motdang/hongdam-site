  • SCB lists signs of a fake transfer slip: letters or numbers in different fonts or thickness, a sender name or time that does not match, and a QR code that cannot be verified. It names the SCB EASY app, where a slip's QR code is scanned, and checkslip.scb.co.th. scb.co.th

  • Under the Revenue Code amendment (No. 48) B.E. 2562, banks and e-payment providers report to the Revenue Department each year a person whose accounts together took 400 or more deposits or incoming transfers totalling ฿2,000,000 or more, or 3,000 or more, in the year. What people ask

How is a transfer slip checked?

SCB names two ways: in the SCB EASY app, scan bill/QR payment and select the transfer slip to verify; or enter the reference code, origin account, amount and the image code on checkslip.scb.co.th. scb.co.th

How can a fake transfer slip be spotted?

SCB, passing on police advice, lists letters or numbers in different fonts or thickness, a sender name or transfer time that does not match, and a QR code that cannot be verified when scanned. It adds that photos of slips posted on social media can be edited by others and used to deceive. scb.co.th

How does a shop take PromptPay?

PromptPay links a citizen ID, a mobile phone number or a bank account number to a bank account. The Bank of Thailand lists PromptPay for Corporate, where a business registers to receive money from customers, and Thai QR Payment, where the buyer scans a code in a banking app. Transfers up to ฿5,000 carry no fee. bot.or.th

What does a slip-checking service cost?

SlipOK lists a free plan of 100 slips a month for up to 2 shops, then ฿350 for 500 slips, ฿600 for 1,000, ฿1,000 for 2,000 and ฿2,580 for 6,000. Annual billing is about 30% cheaper than monthly, and a quote is offered above 200 slips a day. slipok.com

Does the Revenue Department see money received by PromptPay?

Banks and e-payment providers report a person to the Revenue Department when their accounts together took 400 or more deposits or incoming transfers totalling ฿2,000,000 or more, or 3,000 or more, in a year. The report holds the ID number, name, account numbers, the number of transfers and their total value. The department says money in an account is not all income. rd.go.th
