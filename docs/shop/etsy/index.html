What people ask

Can I sell on Etsy from Thailand?

Yes. Etsy lists Thailand among the countries where sellers take Etsy Payments. A seller in Thailand uses a Payoneer account for it. help.etsy.com

What does Etsy charge a seller?

A listing costs a flat US$0.20. Etsy charges 6.5% of the total order amount. The payment processing fee is a set rate plus a percent, and it varies by country. help.etsy.com

How does Etsy money reach a Thai bank?

Etsy deposits US dollars into the seller's Payoneer account. The seller then withdraws to a local bank, which usually takes 2–5 business days. Payoneer may set a minimum for a withdrawal and may charge fees. help.etsy.com

What does eBay charge a seller?

On eBay's selling fees page, for most categories, the first 250 listings a month are free and later ones cost US$0.35. The fee on a sale is 13.6% up to US$7,500, plus US$0.30 an order of US$10 or less, or US$0.40 above that. ebay.com

How does eBay pay a seller?

eBay's page for sellers in South East Asia says payouts go to a bank account, or to a bank account through a Payoneer account. Funds can be paid out daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. International fees apply when eBay converts currency, and they depend on the seller's country of residence. export.ebay.com
