What people ask

How much does a 3-day, 2-night tour of the north cost?

One Chiang Mai operator prices its private 3-day, 2-night Chiang Rai tour from ฿6,100 to ฿11,200 a person, depending on group size. The price has a licensed guide, transport, lunches and entry fees. olsentours.com

Does a tour price include the hotel?

It depends on the operator. The Olsen Tours listing leaves out the two nights in Chiang Rai and lists other meals as not included. olsentours.com

Can a foreign guide lead a tour in Thailand?

No. A tour guide must be a Thai national and permitted under the Tourism Business and Guide Act of 2008. The law on work by foreigners reserves the job for Thai people. so05.tci-thaijo.org

What do foreigners pay to enter the White Temple?

฿200 a person since 1 January 2026, up from ฿100. Thai nationals enter free. chiangraitimes.com
