Good to know

  • A tour guide in Thailand must be a Thai national and licensed under the Tourism Business and Guide Act of 2008. The law on work by foreigners reserves the job for Thai people. so05.tci-thaijo.org
  • The Department of Tourism's website has a lookup that shows whether a tour business or guide is registered. dot.go.th
  • On one Chiang Mai listing for a licensed English-speaking guide, the price covers up to 6 hours of the guide. Transport, food, drinks and entrance fees are not in it, and the traveller pays the guide's own costs when the guide joins meals, activities or transport. wheresidewalksend.com

How much is a car with a driver in Chiang Mai for a day?

How much does a licensed tour guide cost in Chiang Mai?

One Chiang Mai listing for a licensed English-speaking guide starts at US$120 for up to 6 hours. Transport is paid separately. wheresidewalksend.com

Can a foreigner work as a tour guide in Thailand?

No. A tour guide must be a Thai national and permitted under the Tourism Business and Guide Act of 2008. The law on work by foreigners reserves the job for Thai people. so05.tci-thaijo.org

How does someone become a tour guide in Thailand?

The Department of Tourism runs a knowledge and skills test for guides and training courses for guides and tour leaders. Its website lists a notice for applying to the guide test in November 2026. dot.go.th

Who pays the guide's lunch and entry fees?

The traveller. One Chiang Mai guide listing says a guide who joins meals, activities or transport has those costs paid by the traveller. wheresidewalksend.com
