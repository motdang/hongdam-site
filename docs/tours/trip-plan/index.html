Useful facts

  • TUI's Mae Hong Son Loop self-drive tour takes 10 days and about 599 km of driving, through Chiang Mai, Pai, Mae Hong Son and Mae Sariang. It names November to February as the best travel period. tui.co.uk
  • From Chiang Mai, a Chiang Rai day trip is a round trip of about 6 hours on the road. Doi Inthanon is about 2 hours by car. thailandhighlights.com
  • Thai Holiday Guide gives November to January as the best window in Chiang Mai: cool, stable air and clear views. February to April is the burning season, when smoke haze cuts visibility. June to October is rainy. thaiholidayguide.com
  • A tour operator in Thailand needs a valid licence to run tours. The Tourism Business and Guide Act B.E. 2551 (2008) and its amendments, Section 80 with Section 15, sets up to 2 years in prison, a fine of up to ฿500,000, or both. thansettakij.com
  • Loy Krathong falls on Tuesday 24 November 2026. Chiang Mai's city Yi Peng festival runs 23 to 25 November, with the main lantern nights on 24 and 25 November. thaiholidayguide.com

What people ask

What is a good 3-day Chiang Mai itinerary?

Thailand Highlights' 3-day plan gives day 1 to Old City temples or Doi Suthep at sunset, day 2 to an elephant camp programme with a cooking class or a waterfall as an add-on, and day 3 to Doi Inthanon National Park. thailandhighlights.com

How long is a day trip to Chiang Rai from Chiang Mai?

Thailand Highlights gives a round trip of about 6 hours on the road. thailandhighlights.com

When is the best time to visit northern Thailand?

Thailand Highlights calls the dry season, about November to May, the best overall. June to October is the rainy season. thailandhighlights.com

How long does the Mae Hong Son Loop take?

TUI's self-drive tour of the loop takes 10 days and about 599 km: Chiang Mai (days 1–3), Pai (3–5), Mae Hong Son (5–7), Mae Sariang (7–8), then back to Chiang Mai (8–10). tui.co.uk

How much is a private car for a day trip in the north?

A Chiang Mai operator lists ฿4,200 a car for 1–3 people and ฿4,500 a van for 4–10 people, for a 9-hour Chiang Rai day with driver, fuel, insurance and hotel pickup. Entries and food are extra, and a guide is ฿1,500 a day. mychiangmaitour.com
