Good to know

  • A half-day cooking class in Chiang Mai is 3 to 4 hours and 4 dishes, with no market tour. A full-day class is 6 to 8 hours and 5 to 6 dishes, with a market tour. byklo.rent
  • Most cooking schools include hotel pickup and provide aprons, knives, woks and burners. Classes usually have 4 to 12 students. byklo.rent
  • The guide says vegetarian and vegan versions are available in the class formats it covers. Dietary needs go in at booking, at least 48 hours ahead. byklo.rent
  • At Dang Muay Thai in Chiang Mai, a group class runs about 1.5 hours and a private class an hour. fightersvault.com
  • NOVA Artlab in Chiang Mai teaches a full-day silver workshop at 179 Tha Phae Road. Students make a silver ring or pendant by piercing, sawing, filing and polishing. timeout.com

What people ask

How much is a cooking class in Chiang Mai?

The byklo guide lists ฿800 to 1,500 for a half day and ฿1,500 to 2,500 for a full day with a market tour. It says booking directly with a school is 15–30% cheaper than booking through Klook or Viator. byklo.rent

Is there a cooking class in the evening in Chiang Mai?

Yes. The byklo guide lists Smart Cook with a class from 16:30 to 20:30 at ฿800 to 1,200. byklo.rent

Are there vegetarian cooking classes in Chiang Mai?

Yes. The byklo guide says vegetarian and vegan versions are available, and that Smile Organic and Khom Loy specialise in plant-based menus. Tofu, mushroom and tempeh can replace fish sauce and shrimp paste. byklo.rent

How much is a muay thai class in Chiang Mai?

Dang Muay Thai in Chiang Mai lists a single group class at ฿350. A group class runs about 1.5 hours. fightersvault.com

Can a beginner join a muay thai class in Chiang Mai?

Yes. Visit Thailand Today calls The Bear Fight Club in Chiang Mai a small, friendly gym and a beginner favorite, with group classes from about ฿300. visitthailandtoday.com
