What people ask

Which accounting standards does a Cambodian company use?

Emerhub's page names the Cambodian International Financial Reporting Standards (CIFRS), with accounts kept in Khmer and in riel. It adds that US dollars are widely used in daily business. emerhub.com

When are tax returns due in Cambodia?

PwC's summary has monthly returns filed by the 20th of the following month, moved to the next working day when the 20th falls on a weekend or public holiday. The annual return is filed within three months of the tax year-end. taxsummaries.pwc.com

Does a Cambodian company need an audit?

Emerhub says an audit is mandatory for qualified investment projects and public enterprises. Other companies need one when they meet two of three tests: turnover above 4 billion riel, assets above 3 billion riel, or more than 100 employees. emerhub.com

What happens without proper accounting records in Cambodia?

PwC's summary describes a minimum tax of 1% of annual turnover, separate from corporate income tax. It says the tax no longer applies to enterprises that maintain proper accounting records from 1 January 2017, and notes that the law gives no definition of proper accounting records. taxsummaries.pwc.com
