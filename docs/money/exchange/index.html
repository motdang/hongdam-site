Good to know

  • In the Bank of Thailand's notice on authorized money changers (English translation, as amended), an applicant applies to the Minister of Finance through the Bank of Thailand, in March, June, September or December. bot.or.th
  • The same notice has an authorized money changer display its permit, its daily buying and selling rates for each currency it buys or sells, and the words "Authorized Money Changer License No." in conspicuous places in the office. bot.or.th
  • It has the rates for foreign banknotes quoted in line with the average buying and selling rates of authorized juristic persons published by the Bank of Thailand. bot.or.th
  • On 19 August 2026 the Bank of Thailand published draft principles for money changer licensing, with comments open to 18 September 2026. Tilleke & Gibbins reports that they include a pause on new applications through 2027. tilleke.com

What people ask

Does a money changer in Thailand have to show its rates?

The Bank of Thailand's notice has an authorized money changer display its permit and its daily buying and selling rates for each currency it buys or sells, in conspicuous places in the office. bot.or.th

Who can open a money changer in Thailand?

The notice lists a hotel known to foreign guests; a company with ฿10 million paid-up capital in a business such as a large department store or a travel company; a company with ฿1 million capital whose owners and directors are Thai; a duty-free shop; a state body; and Thai-owned sole proprietorships and partnerships at the border or in areas set for tourism businesses. bot.or.th

Does a customer show ID at a money changer?

For a purchase of foreign banknotes worth ฿50,000 or more, the notice has the money changer ask the customer for a copy of an identity document, such as an identification card, a passport or an affidavit. bot.or.th
