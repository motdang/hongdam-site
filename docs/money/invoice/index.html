What people ask

What does a PayPal invoice cost in Thailand?

PayPal Thailand's fee page lists no separate invoicing fee. A payment received on a request for payment counts as a commercial transaction: 3.90% + ฿11 from inside Thailand and 4.40% + ฿11 from outside. paypal.com

What does a Wise invoice cost?

Wise's invoicing article says sending invoices is free and lists no fee for receiving money by bank transfer or via Wise, with exceptions for USD wires and CAD Swift. A card payment carries a fee that depends on the card, up to the equivalent of 10,000 GBP per transfer. wise.com

Is money from abroad taxed in Thailand?

For a Thai tax resident, KPMG's alert says foreign income brought into Thailand from 1 January 2024 is subject to Thai personal income tax, whatever the tax year it was earned in. A later instruction leaves out income earned before that date. kpmg.com

Does an invoice to a customer abroad carry VAT?

Services rendered in Thailand and used outside Thailand are zero-rated, under rules the Director-General sets. A business registers for VAT within 30 days after its turnover passes ฿1.8 million. rd.go.th
