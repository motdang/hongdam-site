Good to know

  • The Labor Protection Act (Council of State translation, 2008) defines an employment contract as a contract made in writing or orally in which a person agrees to work for another, who agrees to pay wages for the work. mekongmigration.org
  • Tilleke & Gibbins writes that Thai law does not require a written employment contract or one in Thai. It mentions bilingual provisions as an option for a Thai employee who reads English poorly. tilleke.com
  • In the Labor Protection Act, a contract with a set period ends when the period ends, with no notice. With no set period, either side ends it by written notice given on or before a pay date, to end at the next pay date, with notice of no more than three months. mekongmigration.org
  • In the Act, wages calculated by the month, day, hour or piece are paid at least once a month, unless otherwise agreed for the employee's benefit. mekongmigration.org
  • From 1 January 2026, social security takes 5% each from employer and employee on wages up to ฿17,500. austchamthailand.com

What people ask

Must an employment contract in Thailand be in writing?

No. Tilleke & Gibbins writes that Thai law has no requirement that an employment contract be in writing, and the Labor Protection Act counts a contract made in writing or orally. tilleke.com

Can a Thai employment contract be in English?

Tilleke & Gibbins says a contract in Thai is not mandatory. It mentions Thai or bilingual provisions as an option for a Thai employee who cannot read English well. tilleke.com

What happens when a fixed-term contract ends?

It ends at the end of the period with no advance notice. Tilleke & Gibbins adds that if the employee keeps working and the employer keeps paying after the end date, the contract becomes open-ended. tilleke.com

What does an employment contract cover?

Tilleke & Gibbins lists the date and parties, the start date, the end date for a fixed term, probation, duties, wages and benefits, working days, hours and holidays, the workplace, confidentiality and termination. tilleke.com
