Good to know

  • In the Labor Protection Act (Council of State translation, 2008), wages calculated by the month, day, hour or piece are paid at least once a month, unless otherwise agreed for the employee's benefit. When an employer ends the employment, the wages due are paid within three days. mekongmigration.org
  • The same Act has wages paid in Thai currency and at the employee's workplace, unless the employee consents to payment by bill or in foreign currency, or at another place or by other means. mekongmigration.org
  • A PromptPay transfer up to ฿5,000 is free. Above that it costs under ฿2 up to ฿30,000, under ฿5 up to ฿100,000 and under ฿10 beyond, and the Bank of Thailand notes that most banks have waived fees for digital payments since 2018. bot.or.th
  • Under the Social Security Act (Council of State translation), an employer remits its own and its employees' contributions within the fifteenth day of the month after the deduction. A late remittance carries a surcharge of 2% a month. sso.go.th
  • From 1 January 2026, social security takes 5% each from employer and employee on wages up to ฿17,500. austchamthailand.com

What people ask

How often must staff be paid in Thailand?

At least once a month for wages calculated by the month, day, hour or piece, unless otherwise agreed for the employee's benefit. This is Section 70 of the Labor Protection Act. mekongmigration.org

Can staff be paid in cash or by bank transfer?

The Labor Protection Act has wages paid in Thai currency at the employee's workplace. The employee's consent opens other places and other means, and payment by bill or in foreign currency. mekongmigration.org

When is social security paid in?

An employer remits its own and its employees' contributions within the fifteenth day of the month after the deduction, with a surcharge of 2% a month on a late remittance. sso.go.th

What does a PromptPay transfer cost?

Up to ฿5,000 it is free. From ฿5,000 to ฿30,000 it costs under ฿2, to ฿100,000 under ฿5, and above that under ฿10. The Bank of Thailand adds that since 2018 most banks have waived fees for digital payments. bot.or.th

What does a payroll service cost in Thailand?
