What people ask

Can foreigners open a bank account in Thailand?

Yes, with the papers a bank lists. KBank's page for opening an account is written for foreigners and residents of Thailand. It asks a foreigner for a passport, a Non-Immigrant visa of any type, and a paper for the reason for the stay, such as a work permit. support.linebk.com

What do foreigners need to open a Thai bank account?

The Thai Visa Services guide lists a passport valid for at least 6 months, a Non-Immigrant visa, proof of a Thai address (a rental contract, a utility bill or a residence certificate from Immigration), a Thai phone number for the codes in banking apps, and an opening deposit of ฿500 to ฿5,000. It says requirements vary by bank and branch. thai-visa-services.com

Can I open a Thai bank account on a visitor visa or a DTV?

KBank's page says accounts are not opened for holders of a visitor visa or another visa meant for travel, such as visa on arrival, visa-free entry or the Destination Thailand Visa (DTV). It says more information is available at a KBank branch, and that it may ask for more papers. support.linebk.com

Which Thai bank is best for foreigners?

The Thai Visa Services guide says Bangkok Bank is widely considered the most foreigner-friendly, with English-speaking staff at main branches in Silom, Sukhumvit and Chiang Mai. It says KBank generally asks for a Non-Immigrant visa and a work permit or residence certificate, and SCB tends to be stricter. It says branch managers have discretion, so branches differ. thai-visa-services.com

Why do retirees open a Thai bank account?

The Immigration Bureau's retirement extension of stay has a route that rests on a deposit of ฿800,000 or more in a commercial bank in Thailand, held at least 2 months before filing and 3 months after permission. The papers include a deposit certificate from that bank. bangkok.immigration.go.th
