What people ask

PayPal account limited: how long does it take to fix?

PayPal's help page gives no set time and says it depends on the complexity of the case. The steps are in the Resolution Center, or the bell icon on the Dashboard. If the account still shows a limit after the steps, PayPal has either reviewed it and emailed for more information, or is still reviewing what was sent. paypal.com

Stripe account frozen or suspended: what happens?

Stripe emails the reason, and the Dashboard shows a red banner. If Stripe has misread the business, the form linked in the email opens the Dashboard, where the account holder adds information and asks for another review. Businesses on Stripe's restricted list are a category it cannot serve. support.stripe.com

Can a shop in Thailand take PromptPay online?

Yes, through a gateway that offers it. Stripe's Thailand pricing lists PromptPay at 1.65% a successful transfer, plus ฿10 for each refund. stripe.com

Who decides on a limited or frozen account?

The provider. A PayPal limitation is lifted through the steps in the account's Resolution Center, and PayPal reviews what is sent. Hongdam prepares the documents for a frozen or limited account, you file the appeal from your own account, and the provider decides. paypal.com

What does a Stripe dispute cost in Thailand?

฿500 for each dispute received, and ฿500 more for each dispute answered by hand, which Stripe refunds when the dispute is won. Smart Disputes costs 30% of the disputed amount for each dispute won. stripe.com
