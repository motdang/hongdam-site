What people ask

How much does a monthly accountant cost in Thailand?

One accountant on Fastwork lists a basic package at ฿3,500–4,500 a month, a standard package at ฿5,500–7,500 that adds payroll and social security, and a premium package at ฿9,000–15,000 that adds advice and analysis. fastwork.co

When does a business register for VAT in Thailand?

Within 30 days after its turnover passes ฿1.8 million. The rate is 7%, and a registered business issues a tax invoice every time a transaction is made. rd.go.th

Does a freelancer file a tax return in Thailand?

A person whose income is not only from employment files a PND 90 return from 1 January to 31 March after the tax year. Filing online adds eight days to the deadline. rd.go.th

What does a Thai tax invoice show?

The words "Tax invoice", the seller's name, address and tax ID, the buyer's name and address, a description with value and quantity, the VAT amount and the date. rd.go.th
