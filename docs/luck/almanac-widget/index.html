Useful facts

  • The Lanna calendar is the northern calendar for finding auspicious times. A good time is called มื้อจั๋นวันดี in northern speech. Sanook says its 2569 (2026) edition marks good and bad days for the 12 months. sanook.com
  • Sanook lists good days such as วันไชยโชค, วันสิทธิโชค and วันมหาสิทธิโชค. They suit starting an auspicious event, buying a house or a car, or a new venture. วันเสีย is a bad day, and Sanook says auspicious events of any kind are left out on it. sanook.com
  • Yi Peng falls on the full moon of the second month of the Lanna lunar calendar, usually in November. The Thailand Foundation says this is the 12th month of the central Thai lunar calendar. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • The Hong Kong Observatory publishes a table of Gregorian and Chinese lunar dates for 1901 to 2100. It also publishes the same data as open data that a program can read. hko.gov.hk
  • The Yijing (I Ching) has 8 trigrams, which are symbols of three lines, and 64 hexagrams, which are symbols of six lines. Straight lines stand for yang and broken lines for yin. plato.stanford.edu

What people ask

What is the Lanna calendar?

Sanook calls it a native calendar of the north. It is used to find auspicious times, such as for a housewarming, a wedding, an ordination or the day a new car is collected. sanook.com

How is a Lanna month different from a Thai month?

The same full moon has two numbers. Yi Peng is the full moon of the Lanna second month. The Thailand Foundation says that night is the 12th month in the central Thai lunar calendar. thailandfoundation.or.th

Which days are good or bad in the Lanna calendar?

Sanook lists good days such as วันไชยโชค, วันสิทธิโชค, วันมหาสิทธิโชค and วันหัวเรียงหมอน, which suits a wedding. Its bad days are วันเสีย, วันมัจจุ, วันวอดวาย and วันไหม้. sanook.com

Can a website show the Chinese lunar date?

The Hong Kong Observatory publishes a conversion table for 1901 to 2100 and the same data as open data. A program can read the open data. hko.gov.hk

What does an I Ching calculator work from?

The Yijing text. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy says its first layer is the 8 trigrams and 64 hexagrams. Its second layer is the hexagram and line statements, attributed to King Wen and the Duke of Zhou in the 11th century BCE. plato.stanford.edu
