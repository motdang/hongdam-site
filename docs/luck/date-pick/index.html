Good to know

  • The Tourism Authority of Thailand writes that couples ask a monk or an astrologer for a lucky day. The couple's birthdays and times of birth are needed for the calculation, and astrologers use lunar calendars to fix wedding days. tatnews.org
  • Sanook's article on new-car dates for 2027 names two good days. Thongchai day (วันธงชัย) stands for victory and success and suits objects and places. Athibodi day (วันอธิบดี) stands for power and standing and suits matters of people. Until the evening of 16 April 2027, Monday is Thongchai day and Saturday is Athibodi day. After that, Thursday is Thongchai day and Sunday is Athibodi day. sanook.com
  • For a house-warming, Sanook's 2027 article weighs the ฤกษ์ of the day, the lunar day (ดิถี) and the banned days together. After 16 April 2027 it prefers Thursday and Sunday, and it names Tuesday and Wednesday as poor days. sanook.com
  • Wan phra, the Buddhist holy days, fall on the 8th and 15th day of the waxing moon and the 8th and 15th day of the waning moon in the Thai lunar month. The exact dates differ a little between calendars. thaipbs.or.th

What people ask

Who picks the date for a Thai wedding?

The Thailand Foundation writes that Thai couples almost always ask an astrologer for a lucky date and time for the engagement and the wedding. Monks join the Buddhist blessing on the wedding day. thailandfoundation.or.th

What else counts when a new-car date is picked?

Sanook's 2027 article says the weekday is one part. The hour (ยาม), the ฤกษ์, the zodiac sign (ราศี), the lunar day (ดิถี) and the owner's birth date count as well. sanook.com

Where can I see wedding dates for 2027?

Thairath publishes a list of good wedding days for each month of 2027. January has the 1st, 3rd, 7th, 14th, 17th, 26th and 30th. The article also names a second way: choosing from the couple's horoscopes, using the birth day, month and year. thairath.co.th

What does a seller need to work out a date?

One Fastwork seller asks for the birth date and birth time of the person, and of both partners for a wedding. The seller also asks for the months wanted, within 3 months, the Thai or Chinese ceremony style, the time of day preferred and an email address. fastwork.co
