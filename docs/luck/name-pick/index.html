Useful facts

  • A person with Thai nationality has a given name and a surname, and may add a middle name. The Department of Provincial Administration says a given name may not match or look like a royal name or title, may not be a vulgar word or have a vulgar meaning, and may not be meant for fraud. stat.bora.dopa.go.th
  • To change a given name, a person with Thai nationality files form ช.1 with the local registrar where the name is on the house registration. The registrar checks the request and issues a certificate of the change (form ช.3). The person then takes it to correct the house registration and asks for a new ID card. stat.bora.dopa.go.th
  • A new surname has no more than 10 consonants. It may not repeat a surname already registered, and it may not have a vulgar word or meaning. stat.bora.dopa.go.th
  • A Fastwork seller of names asks for the current name and the birth day, month and year. The seller reads names by numerology (เลขศาสตร์) and gives the correct English spelling and a time for the change. fastwork.co
