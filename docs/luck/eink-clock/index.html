  • Waveshare's 7.5-inch e-paper display keeps showing its last picture for a long time with the power off. Its page says power is needed mainly to change the picture. waveshare.com
  • The same page lists a black-and-white screen with 4 greys, 800 × 480 pixels, a full refresh in 4 seconds, 26.4 mW to refresh and under 0.01 µA on standby. waveshare.com

What people ask

Does an e-ink screen need power to hold the picture?

Waveshare's page for its 7.5-inch panel says it keeps the last content for a long time with the power down. It lists 26.4 mW to refresh and under 0.01 µA on standby. waveshare.com

How fast does an e-ink screen change?

For Waveshare's 7.5-inch panel the page lists a full refresh in 4 seconds, a fast refresh in 1.5 seconds and a partial refresh in 0.4 seconds. waveshare.com

What runs an e-ink clock?

Waveshare's 7.5-inch panel fits the 40-pin header of a Raspberry Pi. Its page also lists Jetson Nano, Arduino and STM32 boards, over an SPI connection. waveshare.com

Can a clock run on a solar panel?

Waveshare sells a solar power module for 6V to 24V panels. It charges a 3.7V lithium battery and gives 5V or 3.3V at up to 1A. Its page calls it suited to solar-powered, low-power projects. waveshare.com

When is the Lanna New Year?

The Thailand Foundation says Pi Mai Mueang falls near Songkran in April. The official holiday is 13 to 15 April, and 13 April, the first day, is Wan Sungkan Long. thailandfoundation.or.th
