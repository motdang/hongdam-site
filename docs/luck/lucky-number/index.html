What people ask

How can I find out whether a phone number is lucky?

AIS has a tool on its lucky-number page that predicts a phone number, or finds a lucky one, from your birth date. It explains the meaning for money, work, love and health. ais.th

How much does a lucky SIM cost?

AIS lists its lucky-number SIMs at ฿99 or ฿199 a SIM. Names on the list include ซิมเศรษฐีราชาโชค, ซิมเบอร์เรียง ผลรวมดี and ซิมเลขเบิ้ล ผลรวมดี. ais.th

Can I buy a lucky plate number?

Special plate numbers are sold at auction by the Department of Land Transport. In its 20 December 2025 round the starting prices ran from ฿500,000 to ฿1,500,000, for plates of private cars with up to seven seats. autospinn.com

How do I bid for a plate number?

Registration takes a form and an ID card, at the Road Safety Fund office or on tabienrod.com. A company adds its registration certificate, proof of payment and a bank account page. A bid deposit is paid at a bank or in cash. Bids then go in by voice or on the internet on auction day. autospinn.com

Can I bid for a plate online now?

On 4 November 2026 the online auction page at car.tabienrod.com shows a notice. It says the online auction and registration system is closed for maintenance until the site announces that it has reopened. car.tabienrod.com
