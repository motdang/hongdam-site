Useful facts

  • A sak yant is a sacred tattoo tapped into the skin by hand by a monk or a lay master, an ajarn, and then blessed. The Visit Thailand Today guide says a person does not need to be Thai or Buddhist. visitthailandtoday.com
  • At Wat Bang Phra in Nakhon Pathom the monk chooses the design, usually the Gao Yord on the upper back for a first tattoo. People are called in groups of about 20. visitthailandtoday.com
  • A monk may not touch a woman. At Wat Bang Phra women are tattooed with a glove and transparent ink. On temple trips booked through the Sak Yant Chiang Mai office, women are tattooed by an ajarn. visitthailandtoday.com
  • Most masters ask a person to keep the five Buddhist precepts: no killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying or intoxication. Many add their own rules. Breaking them is believed to weaken the blessing. visitthailandtoday.com
  • Thai health rules reported in January 2020 require tattooists to hold a yearly medical certificate. It shows they do not carry hepatitis B or C or tuberculosis. news.cn

What people ask

How much does a sak yant cost in Thailand?

The Visit Thailand Today guide lists an offering set of about ฿100 to ฿150 at Wat Bang Phra. It lists ฿2,000 to ฿3,500 an hour in Chiang Mai town, offering included. Lay masters charge thousands of baht, and elaborate pieces can reach ฿20,000. visitthailandtoday.com

Can a woman get a sak yant from a monk?

The guide says a monk may not touch a woman. At Wat Bang Phra women are tattooed with a glove and transparent ink. On Chiang Mai temple trips women are tattooed by an ajarn. visitthailandtoday.com

Who chooses the design?

At Wat Bang Phra the monk chooses the design, usually the Gao Yord on the upper back for a first tattoo. The guide says there is no say over the design there. visitthailandtoday.com

Do I need to be Buddhist or Thai?

No. The guide says foreigners are now much of the queue at Wat Bang Phra, and the masters expect the rules to be kept like anyone else's. Most masters ask for the five Buddhist precepts. visitthailandtoday.com

When is the Wai Khru festival at Wat Bang Phra?

Once a year, on a Saturday in March set by the lunar calendar. The guide gives 7 March for 2026. Thousands of devotees come to have their tattoos re-blessed. The temple announces each year's date in Thai. visitthailandtoday.com
