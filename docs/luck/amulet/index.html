Useful facts

  • Phuket101 reports that amulets are exempt from Thailand's export licence for Buddha images. Small images, roughly under 12 cm and up to five pieces, pass in practice. Larger images need a licence from the Fine Arts Department. phuket101.net
  • For an export licence the form is Sor Kor 6, with two photos of each piece, a passport copy, the receipt and the piece itself. In Bangkok the Office of National Museums takes about two days. Provincial museums take closer to a week. phuket101.net
  • Thai people say chao phra (เช่าพระ), which means renting or inviting, not buying, a Buddha image. phuket101.net
  • Wikipedia says an amulet's value depends on its look, scarcity, maker, age and reputed powers. It says forgeries are common and rare originals often need an expert's check. en.wikipedia.org

What people ask

Why do Thai people say they rent an amulet?

Phuket101 says Thai people do not call it buying a Buddha image. They say chao phra, which means renting or inviting. The page says the image is not treated as merchandise, and the language reflects that. phuket101.net

Can I take an amulet out of Thailand?

Phuket101 says amulets are exempt from the export licence for Buddha images. It says images roughly under 12 cm, up to five pieces, pass in practice, and larger ones need a licence from the Fine Arts Department. phuket101.net

Are there fake Thai amulets?

Wikipedia says forgeries are common. It says rare originals often need an expert's check, and collectors usually specialise in one type or one temple. en.wikipedia.org

What decides the price of a Thai amulet?

Wikipedia lists its look, scarcity, maker, age and reputed powers. en.wikipedia.org
