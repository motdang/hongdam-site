Useful facts

  • A Bazi chart has four pillars: year, month, day and hour. Each pillar is one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch, so the chart has eight characters. There are ten stems and twelve branches. en.wikipedia.org
  • The hour pillar is set by one of twelve double-hours. Each double-hour belongs to one Earthly Branch and lasts about two hours. en.wikipedia.org
  • The I Ching has 64 hexagrams. A hexagram is six stacked lines, each broken (yin) or unbroken (yang), and two trigrams of three lines make one hexagram. en.wikipedia.org
  • A hexagram is cast with yarrow stalks, which give six numbers from 6 to 9, or with coins. The coin method became widely used in the Tang dynasty and is still used today. en.wikipedia.org

What people ask

What are luck pillars?

Luck pillars, also called major luck cycles (大運, dàyùn), are ten-year periods that add more stem-and-branch influences to the birth chart. Their start and direction are worked out from the month pillar and depend on the person's gender and on whether the year stem is yin or yang. en.wikipedia.org

What do the four pillars stand for?

The year pillar stands for ancestry and early life. The month pillar stands for parents, upbringing and the base of a career. The day pillar stands for the self and the spouse, and its stem is the Day Master, the reference point for the whole chart. The hour pillar stands for children, later life and the inner self. en.wikipedia.org
