Useful facts

  • In a Thai chart, the ascendant (ลัคนา) is the position of the eastern horizon at the time of birth. The chart has twelve houses (ภพ), and the house that holds the ascendant is house 1. th.wikipedia.org
  • The zodiac has twelve signs (ราศี) of 30 degrees each, in a circle of 360 degrees. Most of the planets are bodies of the solar system, and Rahu and Ketu are worked out as the nodes of the moon. th.wikipedia.org
  • In the Thai thaksa system (ทักษา), a person is represented by the weekday of birth. th.wikipedia.org
