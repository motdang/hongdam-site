CCA's donate page sends American donors to Fractured Atlas, its fiscal agent (the New York non-profit that receives gifts for a project), and says gifts there carry 501(c)(3) tax benefits.

Open CCA's donate page
  • Fractured Atlas lists an 8% fee and a US$20,000 online cap.
  • IRS index on ProPublica, 17 Oct 2026: no listing under "Cultural Crossroads Asia".

From 2026, Thai donors deduct gifts from tax only through e-Donation, the Revenue Department's electronic receipt system. A foundation joins it once registered.

  • A search on 17 Oct 2026 found no Thai registered name and no Revenue Department charity listing for CCA.

CCA holds hundreds of textiles, jewelry, instruments and sacred objects of the Akha, Lahu, Lisu, Hmong, Iu Mien and Karen. Digitizing began in spring 2026.

A donor of objects, or of a collection, asks for a deed of gift (the signed paper that transfers ownership) before anything else.
