2. Background

FERC's board agreed on 31 October 2026 to close FERC's accounts with Myriad USA, Myriad Australia and every.org, with the work done directly with the Treasurer. On 3 November 2026 the Treasurer chose to introduce Hongdam to Myriad USA and Myriad Australia by email.

FERC has paid the first half of the fee. This agreement replaces the draft of 6 November 2026.

3. The work

Hongdam will:

  1. Follow Myriad Australia's distribution of FERC's balance, A$7,843.08 on 6 November 2026 (Myriad Australia's minimum is A$7,500), until the money reaches FERC's bank account.
  2. Write to Myriad USA, Myriad Australia and every.org to close FERC's accounts and send any remaining balance to FERC, under each one's own rules and minimums.
  3. Write to the J. Bush Foundation about how its grant for 15 students will reach FERC in 2026 and 2027, so that Myriad Australia can close without losing it.
  4. Prepare the forms, write the emails and do the follow-up.
  5. Copy the Treasurer and nan@motdang.net on every email to Myriad USA, Myriad Australia, every.org and the J. Bush Foundation.
  6. Tell the Treasurer by email when each account is closed and when each payment arrives.

4. What stays with FERC

  1. FERC is the account holder at Myriad USA, Myriad Australia and every.org until each account is closed.
  2. The Treasurer signs every form or instruction that moves money.
  3. Money goes from Myriad USA, Myriad Australia and every.org to FERC's own bank account. Hongdam does not receive FERC's money or have it in any Hongdam account.
  4. The Treasurer introduces Hongdam to Myriad USA and Myriad Australia by email and sends Hongdam the latest balances.
  5. The Treasurer gives Hongdam current sign-in details for the Myriad USA portal, Myriad Australia and every.org, or adds beer@motdang.net as a user where the site allows. The sign-in details in the handover notes of 17 July 2025 are out of date.

  1. ฿9,000 (nine thousand baht) in all. The fee is fixed: it is the same whatever amounts come back.
  2. First half, ฿4,500: paid. Hongdam has received it.

6. Timing

Hongdam starts work on 16 November 2026. The work is finished when all three accounts are closed and their balances have reached FERC, or on 30 June 2027, whichever comes first.

Hongdam does not control how fast Myriad USA, Myriad Australia, every.org or the J. Bush Foundation answer, and is not responsible for their decisions.

7. Ending early

Either side may end this agreement by email, with 14 days' notice. If FERC ends it before Hongdam has written to Myriad USA, Myriad Australia or every.org, Hongdam returns the ฿4,500. If FERC ends it after that, the ฿4,500 stays with Hongdam and the second half is not due.
