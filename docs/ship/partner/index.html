Useful facts

  • From 1 January 2026, Thailand ends its ฿1,500 duty-free limit on goods bought online from abroad. Duty is charged on top of the 7% VAT. forvismazars.com
  • From 29 August 2025, goods worth US$800 or less from all countries lose duty-free de minimis entry into the United States and pay duties, taxes and fees. cbp.gov

What people ask

What is a fulfilment centre in Thailand?

Flash Fulfillment, one Thai operator, lists warehousing, order and inventory software, and shipping with Flash Express. Its five Bangkok warehouses connect to Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada and Tokopedia. It gives prices on request. flashfulfillment.co.th

How much do Shopee and Lazada charge sellers in Thailand?

FlowAccount's August 2026 comparison lists Shopee commissions of 7.49–17.12% for non-Mall shops and Lazada service fees of 6.96–17.12%, by category. Both add a 3.21% transaction or payment fee. flowaccount.com

Do parcels from abroad pay duty in Thailand?

Since 1 January 2026, goods bought online from abroad no longer have the ฿1,500 duty-free limit. Duty is charged on top of the 7% VAT. forvismazars.com

Do parcels sent to the United States pay duty?

Goods worth US$800 or less pay duties, taxes and fees from 29 August 2025. Postal shipments pay a percentage duty or a flat US$80 to US$200 an item; from 28 February 2026 only the percentage method is used. cbp.gov

Can I ship a Buddha image out of Thailand?

It needs a Fine Arts Department permit, shown at Customs. The airport's customs page lists Buddha images, art objects and antiques as restricted goods. donmueang.airportthai.co.th
