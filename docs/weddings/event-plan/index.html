What people ask

How much does a hotel meeting cost per person in Chiang Mai?

Dusit Princess Chiang Mai lists ฿550 net per person for a half day with lunch and one coffee break, and ฿650 for a full day with lunch and two coffee breaks. The room alone is ฿5,000 net for four hours. The offer runs to 31 December 2026. dusit.com

What does a banquet at a riverside resort in Chiang Mai cost?

Anantara Chiang Mai Resort lists a sit-down banquet at ฿1,400–1,800 a person and a buffet at ฿1,600–3,200. A coffee break of six canapés is ฿650 a person. Prices are net, with service charge and tax, from April 2024. anantara.com

How much does an MC cost for a seminar or company dinner?

A Fastwork seller lists ฿5,000 for a seminar or event MC working 6 to 8 hours, with the script written to the client's needs. A booth MC is ฿2,500 for 4 to 6 hours. The seller says the price changes with the place and the time. fastwork.co

How much is a photographer for a company event?

A Fastwork photographer lists ฿1,900 for up to 2 hours, ฿3,000 for a half day and ฿4,500 for up to 10 hours, with unlimited photos. Files arrive through Google Drive. An event in another province is charged as a full-day flat rate. fastwork.co

What does sound and light cost for 50 to 100 guests?

A Fastwork seller lists a small light and sound set at ฿20,000 for one day, for 50 to 100 people. It comes with a sound engineer, a lighting designer, transport, set-up and take-down. fastwork.co

Which months have the fewest rainy days in Chiang Mai?

January has 0.7 rainy days on average, February 0.9, December 1.4 and March 2.3. August has 20.9. The figures are Thai Meteorological Department data for 1981 to 2015. chiangmai.net

When is the smoke season in Chiang Mai?

Crop burning in northern Thailand runs from December to April. In Chiang Mai, PM2.5 averaged 42.5 µg/m³ in January 2021, 47.7 in February and 83.3 in March. The WHO annual target is 10. iqair.com

Is alcohol sold on Buddhist holy days in Thailand?

Alcohol sales stop on Buddhist holy days. Hotels are exempt. nationthailand.com
