Useful facts

  • Most Thai provinces require a foreign partner to bring an affirmation of marital status from their embassy. The British Embassy in Bangkok issues it at an appointment: up to 52 a week, usually on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with waits of up to 3 weeks in peak periods. gov.uk
  • The affirmation and a certified passport copy are translated into Thai by a qualified translator, then legalised by the Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Legalisation offices are in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Pattaya, Phuket and Ubon Ratchathani, and the appointment is booked online. gov.uk
  • The Luxembourg embassy's page lists Thai legalisation at ฿200 a stamp in about 2 working days, or ฿400 a stamp for same-day express service. bangkok.mae.lu
  • The marriage is registered at the district office (amphoe or khet) in the province named on the affirmation, which is valid only for the month and province stated when applying. The registrar issues two identical Thai marriage certificates. A religious ceremony alone has no legal effect. gov.uk
  • The Thai Meteorological Department dates summer from mid-February to mid-May, the rainy season from mid-May to mid-October and winter from mid-October to mid-February. tmd.go.th
  • Agricultural burning in northern Thailand runs from December to April, and the smoke often lasts until Songkran, the Thai New Year. In Chiang Mai, March was the most polluted month from 2017 to 2021; its PM2.5 averaged 83.3 μg/m³ in 2021. iqair.com
  • A traditional Thai wedding day starts with a morning ceremony for immediate family and close friends. It includes a blessing from usually nine monks, the groom's khan maak procession to the bride's house, the dowry (sin sod) presentation and the conch-shell water pouring (rod nam sang). thailandfoundation.or.th

What people ask

How much does a small wedding cost?

KTC's budget guide puts a small, simple wedding at ฿100,000 to ฿300,000 and a medium wedding at a hotel at ฿500,000 to ฿1,000,000. Its line for monks, khan maak trays and the engagement ceremony is ฿20,000 to ฿100,000. ktc.co.th

How much does a wedding planner charge in Thailand?

A directory of Thai planners says studios quote 10–15% of the overall wedding budget, and full destination planning runs US$8,000 to US$35,000. Partial planning and on-site coordination are billed as fixed-fee packages. thewed.com

What does a destination wedding in Thailand cost?

Anantara Chiang Mai Resort lists a Thai wedding at ฿189,000, with chairs for up to 100 guests and two nights in a suite for the couple. A Buddhist offering for nine monks adds ฿18,000, and a sit-down banquet is ฿1,400 to ฿1,800 a person. The brochure prices are valid from April 2024. anantara.com

How does a foreigner marry in Thailand?

The foreign partner gets an affirmation of marital status from their embassy, has it translated into Thai and legalised by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then marries at the district office (amphoe), which issues two Thai marriage certificates. gov.uk

Can the wedding be held at a temple?

Wat Ratchabophit in Bangkok announced on 16 September 2024 that it does not host wedding ceremonies in its grounds. It allows merit-making rites only, with no marriage registration, ring exchange or water pouring. thestandard.co

What does a wedding meal at home cost?

A Chiang Mai caterer lists a buffet from ฿110 a person, a khantoke dinner from ฿170 and a VIP set from ฿200. A Chinese-style table starts at ฿1,800, with table and chairs. wiangpingcatering.com

Which months have the fewest rainy days in Chiang Mai?

In the Thai Meteorological Department's 1981–2015 averages, Chiang Mai has 0.7 rainy days in January, 0.9 in February and 1.4 in December. August has 20.9. chiangmai.net

What is sin sod?

Sin sod is the dowry or bride price. The trays are carried to the bride's house in the groom's khan maak procession and shown to her family. Today it is often symbolic, and the bride's parents may return it to the couple. thailandfoundation.or.th
