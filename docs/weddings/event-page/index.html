What people ask

How much does a ticketing site charge in Thailand?

Peatix lists a Thailand rate of 4.9% plus ฿35 a ticket, in baht, with payment processing included. The fee applies to paid tickets only. help-organizer.peatix.com

Who pays the ticketing fee, the organizer or the buyer?

On Ticketmelon, the organizer picks. If the organizer absorbs the fees, buyers pay exactly the price set. If the fees are passed on, buyers pay that price plus fees. A split can be set in steps of 0.5%. organizer.ticketmelon.com

Can guests add the event to a calendar?

A calendar file in ICS format can be imported into Google Calendar on a computer. Google Calendar accepts ICS and CSV files. support.google.com

Can alcohol be sold on Buddhist holy days?

Most shops, convenience stores and ordinary restaurants must stop selling alcohol on those days. Hotels operating under the hotel law are exempt. In July 2026 the stop ran for two days, from 12.01am on 29 July to midnight on 30 July. nationthailand.com
