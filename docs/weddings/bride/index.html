Good to know

  • In a Thai wedding the morning ceremonies are in Thai traditional style, and the bride and groom usually wear Thai traditional dress. The evening reception is Western: a suit and a white gown. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • A Chiang Mai wedding directory says a couple prepares at least two wedding outfits. More changes are fine. centerwedding.com
  • Chiang Mai shops offer a dress three ways: ready-made hire, hire made to measure (เช่าตัด), and buying made to measure (ตัดซื้อ). centerwedding.com
  • Azazie, a dress retailer, says a bride typically has 2–3 fittings, and that most wedding dress alterations take 6–10 weeks from the first fitting to the final pickup. Heel height affects the hem length. azazie.com
  • Makeup artist Val Garland tells Hello! magazine that some makeup artists are booked up to a year ahead, and suggests a trial about three months before the wedding. Hair stylist Sam McKnight suggests a hair trial about three months ahead, once the dress is known. hellomagazine.com
  • Of the 31 hair and makeup artists on a Chiang Mai wedding directory, 3 list a price. centerwedding.com

What people ask

Where can I hire a wedding dress in Chiang Mai?

A Chiang Mai wedding directory lists dress and suit shops, boutiques and tailors: 20 on its first page. The shops offer ready-made hire, hire made to measure and buying made to measure. centerwedding.com

Is it cheaper to hire or to buy a wedding dress?

At Chiang Mai starting prices, a bridal gown to hire runs ฿3,500 to ฿28,000 and up, and a Thai outfit for the bride starts at ฿1,200. A gown made to measure, hired or bought, runs from ฿10,000 into the hundreds of thousands. centerwedding.com

How much does bridal makeup cost in Chiang Mai?

Of 31 hair and makeup artists on a Chiang Mai wedding directory, 3 list a price. Two list ฿4,000 for makeup and hair together. The third lists bridal makeup from ฿7,500 and bridal hair from ฿2,500. centerwedding.com

How far ahead is a makeup artist booked?

Makeup artist Val Garland tells Hello! magazine that some makeup artists are booked up to a year ahead. She suggests a trial about three months before the wedding, in the morning, so the makeup is worn all day to see how it holds. hellomagazine.com

When should a wedding dress be altered?

Azazie lists the first fitting 8–10 weeks before the wedding, the second 4–6 weeks before and the last 1–2 weeks before. It books the seamstress at least 2–3 months before the wedding. azazie.com

What does a Lanna bride wear?

Mot Dang's Su Khwan page lists a silk tube skirt with a teen jok hem as the bride's Lanna dress. The page offers it with an optional Lanna wedding at a teak house, on the first morning of a Chiang Rai honeymoon. motdang.net

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Chiang Mai?

Of 64 photographers on a Chiang Mai wedding directory, 46 list a one-day price and 36 give a range. The median low is ฿8,500 and the median high ฿17,500. The lowest range starts at ฿3,500 and the highest ends at ฿100,000. centerwedding.com

How much does a bride's bouquet cost?

The add-on list of Anantara Chiang Mai Resort has a bouquet at ฿1,500 and a hand garland at ฿2,500. Its ฿189,000 Western wedding package includes the bride's bouquet and two bridesmaids' corsages. The brochure prices are valid from April 2024. anantara.com
