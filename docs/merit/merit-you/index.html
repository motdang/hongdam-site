What people ask

Can merit be given to someone else?

Thai Buddhist teaching counts sharing merit with others as one of ten kinds of merit-making. Plook Dharma's page calls it pattidanamai and gives dedicating merit and pouring water as examples. trueplookpanya.com

How much is a sangkhathan set?

Thairath reported on 24 July 2026 that sets sell from ฿40 to ฿4,500 a set, depending on the goods packed. Prices were stable against the same period a year earlier. thairath.co.th

Can a gift to a temple be deducted from tax?

For tax year 2569, Thairath says a person deducts only cash gifts made through e-Donation, up to 10% of income after expenses and allowances. A temple counts only when it is legally established in Thailand. thairath.co.th

Which fish can be released, and what is the penalty for the wrong ones?

The Department of Fisheries names native Thai species, among them ปลาตะเพียน (Thai silver barb), ปลาช่อน, ปลาหมอไทย and ปลาสร้อยขาว. Under the Fisheries Emergency Decree B.E. 2558, releasing a non-native species carries up to 2 years in prison, a fine of up to ฿2,000,000, or both. thaipbs.or.th
