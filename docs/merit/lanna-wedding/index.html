Useful facts

  • Khwan is the life essence a person is born with, and Lanna counts 32. A fright, an illness, a fall or a long journey can make it wander. The khwan rite calls it back. motdang.net
  • A mo khwan calls the khwan: usually an older man who was ordained as a monk and left the robe. He reads the call over the finished tray, then ties white cotton thread on the wrists, left first. The thread stays on for about 3 days, until it greys. motdang.net
  • The northern wedding is called mat mue, tying hands. It opens with morning merit-making by the senior relatives of both families, then the families hold a bai si ritual. The couple's hands are tied, and coins of ฿9, ฿12, ฿21 or ฿99, by belief, go into individual bai si bowls. tatnews.org
  • The northern khwan tray, khan bai si, is a silver or lacquer bowl on a footed stand. It holds tiers of banana leaf folded into points, a ball of steamed sticky rice, a peeled boiled egg, a banana, flowers, incense, candles and white cotton thread for the wrists. motdang.net
  • Thai people usually invite nine monks to the Buddhist ceremony, the first part of the wedding morning. The couple offers the monks food. The monks bless the couple and pray over water that is used later in the ceremony. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • Lanna dress for the bride is a silk tube skirt with a teen jok hem, the woven hem of a Lanna tube skirt. The groom wears a silk jacket and pha khao ma. motdang.net
  • For a foreigner, registering at a district office (amphoe) takes an affirmation of freedom to marry from the embassy. The other way is to register at home and hold the rite in Thailand. motdang.net

What people ask

What is a Lanna wedding ceremony?

TAT News calls the northern Thai wedding mat mue, tying hands, and describes it as a simple ceremony rooted in self-sufficiency. It starts with morning merit-making by the senior relatives of both families, then the families hold the bai si ritual. The site adds that younger couples now blend it with central Thai rituals. tatnews.org

What happens in the khwan rite?

A mo khwan calls the khwan of the bride and groom over the bai si tray, which takes about 10–15 minutes. White cotton thread is then tied on the wrists. Mot Dang describes it as a folk rite within Buddhist Thailand, not a Buddhist ordinance. motdang.net

What does Lanna dress hire cost?

One Chiang Mai shop lists hire from ฿280. Depending on the package, an outfit covers the shirt and tube skirt or trousers, plus a sash, belt and accessories. Couple sets are priced on request, and the final price depends on the outfit, the hire period and the accessories. siphatsorncostume.com

Why nine monks?

The Thailand Foundation writes that Thai people usually invite nine monks and does not say why. Thai Rath writes that nine is the highest lucky number in Thai belief, and that the word for nine, kao, sounds like kao, to step forward. thairath.co.th

How is it different from a central Thai wedding?

In the rod nam sang ceremony, guests pour water from a conch shell over the couple's hands while giving blessings and a money envelope. The Thailand Foundation writes that in the north, holy threads are bound around the couple's wrists as an alternative to the shell ceremony. thailandfoundation.or.th

Does a Lanna ceremony make us legally married?

No. Mot Dang's page states that a rite in Thailand is not a legal marriage. A marriage is legal when it is registered at a district office (amphoe). The registration is a separate step from the ceremony. motdang.net
