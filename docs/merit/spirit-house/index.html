Good to know

  • A Shopee Thailand blog puts north-east first for a spirit house, then east, then south-east, and names west and south as forbidden. The house stands on the ground, not inside the home, and on the roof if there is no ground. It is away from a toilet, does not face the front door and does not sit under the eaves. shopee.co.th
  • The same blog gives a time window for each weekday: Sunday 06:09–08:19, Monday 08:29–10:39, Tuesday 06:39–08:09, Wednesday 08:39–10:19, Thursday 10:49–11:39, Friday 06:19–08:09, Saturday 08:49–10:49. It bars some weekdays in some lunar months. It does not bar Sunday, which it calls a strong day. shopee.co.th
  • Thaiger says a religious ceremony, usually led by a Brahman priest, invites the spirits to live in the house, after the house is positioned and decorated. It lists places that rule a spot out: the shadow of the building, the left side of a door, facing a toilet and facing a road. Ideally the house faces north or north-east. thethaiger.com
  • Shopee's blog lists a standard offering of 25 items, among them a pig's head, a boiled chicken, a duck, a steamed fish, five kinds of sweets in pairs of plates, two pineapples, two hands of bananas, a bottle of liquor and a pair of bai si trays topped with an egg. It also lists a vegetarian set of 20 items. shopee.co.th

What people ask

Which way should a spirit house face?

A Shopee Thailand blog puts north-east first, then east, then south-east. It names west and south as forbidden. shopee.co.th

Which day and time suit a spirit house?

The blog gives a time window for each weekday, for example Sunday 06:09–08:19 and Saturday 08:49–10:49. It bars Thursday and Saturday in lunar months 1, 5 and 9, Wednesday and Friday in months 2, 6 and 10, Tuesday in months 3, 7 and 11, and Monday in months 4 and 8. shopee.co.th

How much does a spirit house cost?

On Yellow Pages one seller lists a dragon-pillar set with two front tables at ฿10,900, a naga-pillar set at ฿12,900 and a medium set at ฿17,900. These are listings for the shrine and its tables. yellowpages.co.th

How many incense sticks go to a spirit house?

Thairath's article of 9 September 2024 gives 9 incense sticks for a spirit house. For a grandparents' shrine (ศาลตายาย) it gives 5 sticks and 2 candles. thairath.co.th

Who performs the ceremony?

Shopee's blog says a Brahman priest is invited to inspect the site and set the shrine up. It says a Brahman priest also holds the rite when a spirit house is removed. shopee.co.th
