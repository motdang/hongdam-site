Good to know

  • Merit on the 7th, 50th and 100th days can fit into one day: the host invites 5, 7 or 9 monks to chant, then serves breakfast or lunch the same day. It can also take two days, with chanting in the evening and a meal the next day. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • The family of the person who died decides whether to invite guests to the ceremony on these days. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • Thai gives each day a name: สัตมวาร for the 7th, ปัญญาสมวาร for the 50th and ศตมวาร for the 100th. Merit is made and dedicated to the person who died, and monks are invited to chant and to eat the midday meal. trueplookpanya.com
  • Merit for the dead is made on the 7th, 50th and 100th days. so06.tci-thaijo.org

What people ask

From which day do you count the 100 days?

KTC's page on the 100th-day merit counts the day of death as day 1. The merit falls on day 100. ktc.co.th

How many monks are invited?

The Thailand Foundation says the host invites 5, 7 or 9 monks. KTC's page says at least 5, in an odd number. thailandfoundation.or.th

What is prepared for the 100th day?

KTC's page lists a sangkhathan set (a set of everyday goods offered to monks), flowers, incense and candles, sacred string, a vessel for pouring water, food for the monks, rice for the Buddha, rice for the person who died, and a photo or the ashes. It says a temple can lend many of these items. ktc.co.th

Can the merit be made before or after day 100?

KTC's page says the merit falls on day 100, with no day earlier and none later. It gives a belief about the person who died as the reason. ktc.co.th

What colour do people wear?

KTC's page names muted colours such as cream, grey, dark navy or white. It says black or all white is not needed. ktc.co.th
