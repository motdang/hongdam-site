Good to know

  • A house blessing invites an odd number of monks, such as 5, 7 or 9. The host calls the temple first to confirm the date and the number of monks. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • The monks' lunch is held in the daytime, at about 11:00, and ends by 12:00. Theravada monks may not eat after noon. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • The order: the monks arrive and chant; the host offers lunch to the Buddha statue and the monks; after the meal the host may present flowers, incense, candles or a sangkhathan set; the monks chant again and sanctify the space with holy water. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • The host decorates the building with holy thread (sai sin) and circles it round a Buddha statue and the monks' seats. The monks write yantra in powder and press gold leaf at significant places, including on or above the front door. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • In the North, the host also hires a ceremonial master to lead the proceedings. Business owners may hold the ceremony for new offices. thailandfoundation.or.th
  • Chiang Mai has 0.7 rainy days in January and 20.9 in August, on average over 1981–2015. chiangmai.net

What people ask

How many monks do I invite?

The Thailand Foundation names an odd number, such as 5, 7 or 9. The host calls the temple first to confirm the date and the number of monks. thailandfoundation.or.th

What happens at the ceremony, in order?

The monks arrive, sit and chant. The host offers lunch to the Buddha statue and the monks. After the meal the host may present flowers, incense, candles or a sangkhathan set. The monks chant again and sanctify the space with holy water. thailandfoundation.or.th

What time of day is the ceremony?

KTC names two common times: 7:30 in the morning, or the monks' lunch at 10:30. ktc.co.th

What do I need to prepare?

KTC lists an altar table with a Buddha image, a sangkhathan set, the monks' envelope and daily necessities, a holy-water bowl with silver and gold leaf, flowers, incense, candles, food, drinking water, mats and chairs for guests. ktc.co.th

How much does a house blessing cost?

One Bangkok household budgeted about ฿14,000 in 2020 for 5 monks and 40 guests. Food and drink came to ฿8,436, the monks' envelope to ฿3,000 and the sangkhathan set to ฿1,500. sanook.com

How far ahead are the monks invited?

KTC puts the lead time at about one month. ktc.co.th

Which day is a good day?

Many people look at the Chinese calendar, for example Thongchai days, says KTC. Others pick a weekend when relatives can come. ktc.co.th

Do shops hold the ceremony too?

The Thailand Foundation notes that business owners may hold the ceremony for new offices. thailandfoundation.or.th
