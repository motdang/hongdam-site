What people ask

Where can I find an English-speaking doctor in Chiang Mai?

Expatica names Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai, Rajavej Chiang Mai Hospital and Dr Artima Medical Clinic, a Thai and Australian qualified GP who consults in English and Thai. It adds that English support varies between the doctor, the reception and the pharmacy. expatica.com

How much does a specialist visit cost in Chiang Mai?

Expatica estimates about ฿300 to ฿500 for a specialist at a public hospital and ฿2,000 to ฿4,000 or more at an international hospital. Lab work, imaging and medicines are extra. expatica.com

How much does a dentist cost?

Bangkok Hospital's headquarters lists a check-up and planning at ฿350 to ฿850, scaling and polishing at ฿1,800 to ฿2,700, a tooth extraction at ฿1,600 to ฿3,800 and a composite filling at ฿2,000 to ฿6,300 a tooth. The prices are valid until 31 December 2026. bangkokhospital.com

Do foreigners pay a deposit at a Chiang Mai hospital?

Expatica says private hospitals may ask foreign patients to show a passport and pay a deposit or a card hold before treatment. Some insurers pay the hospital directly; others need the patient to pay first and claim later. expatica.com
