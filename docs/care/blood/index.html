What people ask

Do I fast before a blood test, and can I drink water?

Thai PBS gives at least 8 hours without food for a blood sugar test and 10 to 12 hours for a lipid test. Plain water is allowed, citing Phyathai Hospital. Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai lists 10 to 12 hours with small sips of water for its check-up. thaipbs.or.th

How much does a health check-up cost in Chiang Mai?

Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai lists its Essential Medical Program for men and women at ฿6,900, down from ฿10,000. The price is valid until 31 December 2026, and the service can be used until 31 January 2027. bangkokhospital.com

What does a basic check-up include?

At Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai the 16-item program has a physical exam, an EKG, a chest X-ray, a urine test, a report book and these blood tests: blood count, fasting sugar, HbA1c, cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglyceride, uric acid, creatinine with GFR, liver (SGPT), vitamin D, HBsAg and HBsAb. bangkokhospital.com

Do foreigners pay more than Thais for a check-up?

The terms of the Essential Medical Program at Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai say its price applies to both Thai nationals and expatriates. bangkokhospital.com
