Good to know

  • The compulsory motor insurance pays actual treatment costs up to ฿30,000 per person before fault is decided. Once the driver is shown to be liable, the limit is ฿80,000 per person, with the first ฿30,000 included. thaipbs.or.th
  • For death or total permanent disability the compulsory insurance pays ฿35,000 per person before fault is decided, and up to ฿500,000 once liability is shown. A patient in hospital gets ฿200 a day for up to 20 days. thaipbs.or.th
  • The compulsory insurance covers any person harmed by a vehicle: owner, driver, passenger or pedestrian, in or out of the vehicle, whether or not at fault. Cover starts at the moment of the accident. oiceservice.oic.or.th
  • A request for the initial payment is made within 180 days of the harm. The initial payment comes without waiting for fault to be proved. oiceservice.oic.or.th
  • The Fund pays within 7 days of a request with complete papers when the vehicle was uninsured, the driver drove off, or the vehicle cannot be identified. oiceservice.oic.or.th
  • Under the Cabinet's revised UCEP rules of 15 September 2026, a hospital treats a critical emergency patient until the patient is stable and for up to 72 hours from admission, with treatment not conditional on payment. Motor accident protection and insurance are used first. thestar.com.my
  • WHO estimates 18,218 road deaths in Thailand in 2021, an average of 50 a day, a rate of 25.4 per 100,000 people. Motorcyclists were 83.8% of all traffic deaths. who.int

What people ask

What does the compulsory insurance (Por Ror Bor) pay after a motorcycle accident?

It pays actual treatment costs up to ฿30,000 per person before fault is decided, and up to ฿80,000 once the driver is shown to be liable. For death or total permanent disability it pays ฿35,000 first and up to ฿500,000 once liability is shown. thaipbs.or.th

Who does the compulsory insurance cover?

The Office of the Insurance Commission's summary says it covers any person harmed by a vehicle: an owner, a driver, a passenger or a pedestrian, in or out of the vehicle. It applies whether or not the person was at fault, from the moment of the accident. oiceservice.oic.or.th

How long is there to ask for the initial payment?

180 days from the day of the harm. The initial payment is made without waiting to prove who was at fault. oiceservice.oic.or.th

What if the other vehicle drove off or had no insurance?

The Fund (กองทุนทดแทนผู้ประสบภัย) pays the initial amount. It pays within 7 days of receiving the request and complete papers, and can claim the money back from the vehicle owner or insurer. oiceservice.oic.or.th

Which papers does a hospital ask for in a compulsory-insurance claim?

Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai lists the original police report; a copy of an ID card or driving licence; a copy of the policy, registration, purchase contract or tax sticker; and its accident confirmation form. A driver who is not the injured person, and the vehicle owner, have their own lists. bangkokhospital.com

Will a hospital treat me before I pay?

Under the revised UCEP rules of 15 September 2026, a hospital treats a critical emergency patient without making treatment conditional on payment, for up to 72 hours from admission. The patient pays in two cases: refusing a transfer once no longer critical, or choosing another facility. thestar.com.my

Which number calls an ambulance?

1669. The website of the National Institute for Emergency Medicine lists it for a medical emergency (เจ็บป่วยฉุกเฉิน โทร.1669). niems.go.th

How many people die on Thai roads?

WHO estimates 18,218 road deaths in 2021, an average of 50 a day. Motorcyclists were 83.8% of all traffic deaths. who.int
