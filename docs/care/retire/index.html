Good to know

  • Care facilities for older people or people who depend on care come under the Health Business Establishments Act 2016, its ministerial regulations and their operating licences. A 2020 ministerial regulation sets premises, safety and service standards. thestar.com.my
  • 1,203 care facilities were registered with the Department of Health Service Support in October 2026. The government announced on 11 October 2026 that the Ministry of Public Health directs on-site inspections of the registered facilities within two months. The checks cover premises, safety systems and qualified staff. thestar.com.my
  • Thailand separates day care, residential care and nursing-home services into different categories. chiangraitimes.com
  • A care home usually asks one month as a deposit, and some ask a fixed ฿20,000. On signing a contract, two months are paid in advance. thailandstarterkit.com
  • KBank lists for a foreigner: a passport, a government ID from the home country (a national ID card, driver's licence or household registration), and a paper for the reason for the stay, such as a work permit or a school certificate. support.linebk.com
  • SCB's deposit FAQ lists an ID card for opening a savings account, and says a foreigner uses a passport and a work permit. scb.co.th

What people ask

How much does a care home in Chiang Mai cost?

At Ban Sabai Village in Chiang Mai, a standard room with breakfast is ฿67,000 to ฿73,000 a month. Care is billed separately: ฿27,000 for shared care 3 to 4 hours a day, ฿130,000 for one-to-one care around the clock. thailandstarterkit.com

How much does a caregiver cost?

ElderThai, an agency, lists ฿1,200 for 4 hours, ฿2,200 for 8 hours and ฿3,000 for 24-hour cover. On the same page, hiring a helper privately is about ฿30,000 a month. elderthai.com

Is there a government care home in Chiang Mai?

The Thammapakorn Social Welfare Development Center for Older Persons is in Chiang Mai, and its website carries the copyright of the Department of Older Persons. It links a fact-finding form for older persons who wish to use its services, and staff can be reached on 053-278573 and 053-281206. thammapakorn.dop.go.th

Can foreigners open a bank account in Thailand?

Yes, with the papers a bank lists. KBank's page for opening an account is written for foreigners and residents of Thailand. It asks a foreigner for a passport and a paper for the reason for the stay, such as a work permit. support.linebk.com

How much does a month in Chiang Mai cost?

Numbeo's contributors put a 1-bedroom outside the centre at ฿8,000 to ฿15,000 a month, and in the centre at ฿12,000 to ฿24,000. numbeo.com

What does health insurance cost after 60?

In Chubb Samaggi's long-stay plan with a deductible, the first-year net premium at ages 61–70 is ฿7,700 to ฿26,400, and at 71–80 it is ฿11,400 to ฿39,600, across three plans with inpatient limits of ฿400,000 to ฿600,000. chubb.com
