Good to know

  • An elderly care home needs a health establishment licence from the Department of Health Service Support, and the person who manages it needs a separate licence. Running an unlicensed home carries up to six months in jail, a fine of up to ฿50,000, or both. thailandstarterkit.com

What people ask

How much does a caregiver cost in Thailand?

One Bangkok agency lists ฿1,200 for a four-hour visit, ฿2,200 for an eight-hour day and ฿3,500 a day for 24-hour live-in care. A full-time nurse costs around ฿37,500 a month. The page says agencies typically charge 1.5 to 2 times the cost of hiring a caregiver directly. thailandstarterkit.com

Does a care home in Thailand need a licence?

Yes. It needs a health establishment licence from the Department of Health Service Support, and the manager needs a separate licence. A company registration certificate is not a licence. Thai law sets no minimum staff-to-resident ratio. thailandstarterkit.com

Who do I call if an older person in Thailand is neglected or abused?

The Department of Older Persons' Chiang Mai center says reports go to the MSDHS hotline 1300, open around the clock, or to the Department of Older Persons' website. thammapakorn.dop.go.th

How much is a care home in Chiang Mai?

Ban Sabai Village lists a standard room at ฿67,000 to ฿73,000 a month with breakfast, and care billed on top: ฿27,000 for shared care of 3 to 4 hours a day, ฿44,000 for up to 8 hours and ฿130,000 for one-on-one care around the clock. The page says signing can mean paying two months in advance. thailandstarterkit.com
