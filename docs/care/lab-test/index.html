Useful facts

  • The Bureau of Laboratory Accreditation at Thailand's Department of Science Service has accredited testing laboratories under ISO/IEC 17025 since 2003. It publishes a list of accredited testing laboratories, and lists of withdrawn and suspended ones. dss.go.th
  • The bureau has been a signatory of the APLAC mutual recognition arrangement since 22 May 2006 and of the ILAC arrangement since 23 August 2006. dss.go.th
  • A Thai university testing centre's quotation form for drinking water lists 16 lines, from colour and pH to E. coli, for ฿3,040 in total. It gives delivery within 30 days of the agreement and holds the price for 90 days. civil.eng.nu.ac.th
  • The central research unit of Mahidol University's Faculty of Pharmacy offers NMR analysis to schools, government bodies, state enterprises and private clients. State enterprises and private clients pay more than government bodies and schools, and the solvent is charged on top. pharmacy.mahidol.ac.th

What people ask

How much does a water test cost in Thailand?

A Thai university testing centre quotes ฿3,040 for 16 lines on drinking water. Single lines run from ฿60 for pH to ฿330 for total coliforms or E. coli. civil.eng.nu.ac.th

What does a drinking-water test cover?

The Naresuan University form lists colour, pH, turbidity, total hardness, total solids, suspended solids, dissolved solids, nitrate, chloride, fluoride, sulfate, manganese, iron, total coliforms and E. coli, with a sample digestion line. civil.eng.nu.ac.th

How do I find an accredited lab in Thailand?

The Bureau of Laboratory Accreditation at the Department of Science Service publishes a list of accredited testing laboratories. It has accredited testing labs under ISO/IEC 17025 since 2003, and it lists withdrawn and suspended ones too. dss.go.th

How long does a lab test take?

It depends on the lab. The Naresuan University form gives delivery within 30 days of the agreement and holds its price for 90 days. civil.eng.nu.ac.th

What does it cost to test a compound's purity?

At Mahidol University's Faculty of Pharmacy, one 1H NMR sample costs ฿700 for a private client and ฿400 for government bodies and schools, with the solvent extra. 13C NMR is ฿800 an hour for a private client. The price sheet is a file made in 2014. pharmacy.mahidol.ac.th
