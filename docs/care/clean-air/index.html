Useful facts

  • Chiang Mai's burning season runs about February to April, with March the worst month, and the rains usually arrive in May. Open burning was banned from 1 January to 31 May 2026. thailandstarterkit.com
  • Thailand's daily PM2.5 limit is 37.5 µg/m³ and the WHO guideline is 15 µg/m³. On the morning of 1 April 2026 the 24-hour average was 80.3 at Suthep and 110.3 at Chang Phueak in Chiang Mai city, and 286.0 at Muang Na in Chiang Dao. thailandstarterkit.com
  • The EPA's sizing table asks for a CADR of 130 for a room of 200 sq ft (about 19 m²) and 195 for 300 sq ft (about 28 m²), worked out for an 8-foot ceiling. epa.gov
  • The EPA says manufacturers typically recommend replacing filters every 60 to 90 days. It says CADR covers particles only, and for gases it points to an activated carbon or other gas-removing filter. epa.gov
  • Live PM2.5 readings come from IQAir, from Air4Thai (the Pollution Control Department's official network, which shows a 24-hour average) and from Chiang Mai University's DustBoy sensors, which show local changes in real time. thailandstarterkit.com

What people ask

When is the burning season in Chiang Mai?

About February to April, with March the worst month. The rains usually arrive in May. Open burning was banned from 1 January to 31 May 2026, outside designated fuel management zones. thailandstarterkit.com

What is Thailand's PM2.5 limit?

The daily limit is 37.5 µg/m³, and the WHO guideline is 15 µg/m³. The page quotes Berkeley Earth's rule of thumb that 22 µg/m³ over a full day is roughly one cigarette. thailandstarterkit.com

What size air purifier do I need?

The EPA's table gives the minimum CADR for a room: 65 for 100 sq ft, 130 for 200, 195 for 300, 260 for 400, 325 for 500 and 390 for 600, for an 8-foot ceiling. For a higher ceiling it points to a cleaner sized for a larger area, and for an open plan it counts the whole space. epa.gov

How often do air purifier filters change?

The EPA says manufacturers typically recommend replacement every 60 to 90 days, and sooner when a filter looks heavily soiled at replacement. It says a dirty, overloaded filter does not work well. epa.gov
