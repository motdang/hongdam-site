Good to know

  • A death outside a hospital is reported to the local police as soon as possible. The police report is needed to register the death at the district office (amphur). gov.uk
  • A hospital's confirmation of death is not an official death certificate. The amphur writes the certificate in Thai and issues one original to the person who reports the death. gov.uk
  • After a death outside a hospital or by accident, the police investigate and may ask for an autopsy. The Australian Embassy says the autopsy report can take at least 6 to 8 weeks. thailand.embassy.gov.au
  • The German Embassy in Bangkok says preparing the flight home for a body or an urn takes about 1 to 3 weeks, and that cremation is usual in Thailand for cultural reasons. bangkok.diplo.de
  • The Australian Embassy gives next of kin a letter to release the remains from the hospital and cancels the passport. It does not investigate deaths, and it does not provide or certify translations. thailand.embassy.gov.au

What people ask

What happens first when someone dies in Thailand?

A hospital usually gives the family a notice of death. The family registers the death and receives the certificate at the district office (amphur or tedsaban). After a death outside a hospital or by accident, the police investigate and may ask for an autopsy. The Australian Embassy suggests the family engage a funeral director. thailand.embassy.gov.au

How much does it cost to send a body home from Thailand?

Three funeral firms on the Australian Embassy's list estimate ฿100,000 to ฿125,000 to fly a body to Australia, against ฿31,000 to ฿41,000 for a cremation in Thailand with the ashes released. The figures date from January 2026. thailand.embassy.gov.au

Does insurance pay for sending a body home?

The German Embassy says that if the person was insured for a death abroad, the insurer pays, under its terms, either for a burial in Thailand or for the return of the body or urn, and appoints a funeral firm. The family tells the insurer which it wants. Without cover, the family contacts a funeral firm itself and usually pays the costs. bangkok.diplo.de

How long does it take to send a body home?

The German Embassy puts the preparation at about 1 to 3 weeks. It says the funeral at home is set only after the funeral firms in Thailand and in Germany have confirmed the transfer dates. bangkok.diplo.de

Is the Thai death certificate in English?

No. The amphur writes it in Thai and issues one original to the person who reports the death. For use in the UK it needs translating, and the UK government page suggests certification by the Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs. gov.uk
