What people ask

Where can I complain about a shop or a seller in Thailand?

The Consumer Protection Board's FAQ page lists the hotline 1166, the OCPB Connect app, www.ocpb.go.th, and the provincial hall or Damrongdhama Center of a province. ocpb.go.th

Can a foreigner complain to the Consumer Protection Board?

Yes. The English section of the OCPB's FAQ page says a foreigner can submit a complaint personally at the Complaint Center in Bangkok, online or by post. It lists 1166 for consultation. ocpb.go.th

Can a landlord charge any price for electricity and water?

The Consumer Protection Board's 2025 notification on residential leases says water and electricity charges must not exceed the rates set by the state. The Board suggested ฿4.88 a unit as the electricity cap. ocpb.go.th
