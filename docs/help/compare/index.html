What people ask

How much does it cost to have prices compared?

One Fastwork seller lists ฿590 to compare 2 factories, ฿1,200 for 5 and ฿2,500 for 10, and says the fee is a service fee, not included in the product price. fastwork.co

What health insurance does a retirement extension need?

For holders of an O-A visa, the Immigration Bureau's page lists cover of at least US$100,000 or ฿3,000,000 for the whole stay. If insurance is denied, the page says a deposit of at least ฿3,000,000 may be required instead. bangkok.immigration.go.th

How do I compare air conditioners for electricity use?

EGAT's page names the No. 5 energy-saving label with stars on air conditioners. egat.co.th

How do I find out a condo's foreign quota?

The Thaiger's property guide says the 49% foreign quota is counted per building, and that the building's management entity gives written confirmation of it. thethaiger.com
