Good to know

  • For an address outside the US, VA.gov names two ways to verify: ID.me or CLEAR. On the ID.me screen there is an option for people who don't live in the US, and the check is a video call with an ID.me representative. va.gov
  • VA.gov accepts ID.me from abroad; without a US Social Security number, ID.me with a US passport is the route. va.gov
  • On the ID.me video call, the agent checks the original identity documents; copies or photos are not accepted. Calls are offered in over 240 languages, with a third-party interpreter for people who don't speak English. help.id.me
  • ID.me also takes a valid non-US passport together with a document that shows the international address, such as a bank statement or a utility bill. help.id.me

What people ask

How do I verify with ID.me from overseas?

VA.gov says that with an address outside the US you can use ID.me or CLEAR. The ID.me screen has an option for people who don't live in the US. ID.me then asks for your identity documents on a video call with a representative. va.gov

Can I use ID.me without a US Social Security number?

VA.gov says that without a US Social Security number, ID.me is the way to verify. A valid US passport is one of the documents it asks for. va.gov

Can a family member with a non-US passport verify?

ID.me's help page lists a valid non-US passport and a document that shows the international address, such as a bank statement or a utility bill. The person confirms they were never issued an SSN or ITIN, uploads both documents, then joins the video call with the originals in hand. help.id.me

What if ID.me verification is not working?

ID.me's help page says that when benefits are blocked, the agency itself is the one to contact, since some agencies may require an SSN for identity verification. help.id.me

Does help with a VA claim cost money?

VA.gov says the services of an accredited VSO representative on VA benefit claims are free. An accredited attorney or claims agent can charge fees. va.gov
