Good to know

  • The Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists 191 for general emergencies, 1155 for the “Tourist Police”, 199 for fire and rescue and 1669 for emergency medical services. mfa.go.th
  • The Office of the Consumer Protection Board takes advice and complaints on 1166. Its FAQ page says a foreigner can also submit a complaint personally at its Complaint Center in Bangkok, online or by post. ocpb.go.th
  • The Provincial Waterworks Authority (PWA) gives 1662 for a report of no water or low pressure; its page also links a report form. pwa.co.th
  • The Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) gives 1129 as its Contact Center number, with a live chat, and links a Power Outage Notification page. pea.co.th

What people ask

Can someone call a Thai office for me?

Fastwork lists sellers who answer calls, schedule appointments and coordinate for a client. One listing shows a starting price of ฿300. fastwork.co

What number do I call when the water stops?

The Provincial Waterworks Authority's page gives 1662 for no water or low pressure, and a link to report online. pwa.co.th

What number reaches the electricity authority?

The Provincial Electricity Authority's page gives 1129 as the PEA Contact Center, and a live chat for 1129. pea.co.th

How do I make a consumer complaint by phone?

The Office of the Consumer Protection Board gives 1166. Complaints also go through its OCPB Connect app and at the provincial hall or Damrongdhama Center of a province. ocpb.go.th
