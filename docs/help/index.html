Useful facts

  • The Department of Consular Affairs legalises documents (confirms the stamp or signature on them) by post for ฿200 each. consular.mfa.go.th
  • VA.gov (the US veterans' website) accepts ID.me sign-in from other countries. Without a US Social Security number, a person signs in to ID.me with a US passport. va.gov
  • Online 90-day reports (your address, sent to immigration) stop working after a passport change. The report is then made in person or by someone you authorise. immigration.go.th
