What people ask

Does a landlord have to report a foreign tenant to immigration?

The Immigration Bureau's page names landlords and house owners who accommodate foreign nationals on a temporary basis. The notice is due within 24 hours of arrival, to the local immigration office or, where there is none, the local police station. immigration.go.th

Can a landlord charge any price for electricity and water?

The Consumer Protection Board's 2025 notification on residential leases says water and electricity charges must not exceed the rates set by the state. The Board suggested ฿4.88 a unit as the electricity cap. ocpb.go.th

Where can I complain about a landlord?

The Consumer Protection Board's FAQ lists its hotline 1166, the OCPB Connect app, the website complaint system, and the provincial hall or Damrongdhama Center of a province. A foreigner can also submit a complaint personally in Bangkok, online or by post. ocpb.go.th

What does an interpreter cost?

One Fastwork seller lists an online interpreter package from ฿800, ฿2,500 for a half day of up to 4 hours and ฿5,000 for a full day of up to 8 hours. The listing says on-site work is limited to Bangkok and nearby, with travel not included. fastwork.co
