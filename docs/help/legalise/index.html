What people ask

How much does legalisation cost in Thailand?

The Department of Consular Affairs' postal service charges ฿200 a document, paid by money order. The return goes by EMS, with the postage paid in advance in stamps. consular.mfa.go.th

Does Thailand issue apostilles?

Thailand deposited its accession to the Apostille Convention on 30 June 2026. After an 8-month period for objections from member states, the Department of Consular Affairs gives 28 February 2027 as the date it takes full effect, and expects joining to simplify legalisation. consular.mfa.go.th

Can someone else send my document for legalisation?

Yes. The postal service lists a power of attorney for someone sending on the owner's behalf. For foreigners it asks for copies of the ID or passport and the latest Thai entry visa, for both the person giving the power and the person holding it. consular.mfa.go.th

Is a translation needed for legalisation?

The postal checklist includes an English or Thai translation certified as correct by the translator. From 10 February 2026, translators also attach a copy of a valid government-issued ID. consular.mfa.go.th
