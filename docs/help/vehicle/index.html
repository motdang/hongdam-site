Useful facts

  • For a foreign owner, the Department of Land Transport's registration rule asks for a passport (or an alien identity certificate) with a photo, and one of these: a certificate of residence under the immigration law, a document showing a home in Thailand issued by a Thai authority, a foreign government body or an international organisation, or a work permit with a photo. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • A foreign owner with no domicile in Thailand files at the registry for the place shown in the official residence document, and only there. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • A transfer is notified to the registry within 15 days of the sale. Seller and buyer sign the transfer form, and the vehicle is brought in for inspection, except for a hire-purchase transfer. The vehicle carries no unpaid tax and no registry hold. The fees are ฿5 for the form and ฿100 for the transfer, and the service takes one working day. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • For a final sale the transfer needs the registration book, proof of the sale such as a receipt or a sale contract, and the ID of seller and buyer (for a foreigner, the alien identity certificate). The officer inspects the vehicle, confirms the stamp duty is paid, writes the transfer in the book and hands the book back. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • The registration book (ใบคู่มือจดทะเบียนรถ, the green book) is where the registry writes each transfer and each change to the vehicle, such as colour, engine or fuel. It has a box for yearly tax payments; when the box is full, a new book is issued for a fee. With no book, the entry reads “no book used” and a certified print-out of the registry data costs ฿20. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • The yearly tax on a private motorcycle is ฿100. A private car of up to 7 seats pays by engine size: ฿0.50 a cc for the first 600 cc, ฿1.50 a cc from 601 to 1,800 cc and ฿4 a cc above 1,800 cc. From the sixth year of registration the tax falls by 10%, and by 50% in the tenth year. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th
  • A car (sedan, van or pickup) registered for 7 years or more, and a motorcycle registered for 5 years or more, must pass an inspection at a private inspection station before the yearly tax. A private vehicle’s tax can be paid up to 90 days ahead, and proof of compulsory insurance goes with the payment. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

What people ask

Can a foreigner buy a car in Thailand?

The Department of Land Transport’s registration rule has a clause for foreign owners. The owner gives a passport or alien identity certificate with a photo, and a certificate of residence under the immigration law, a document showing a home in Thailand from an authority or organisation, or a work permit. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

Can a short-stay visitor buy a car in Thailand?

The rule lists documents and names no visa type. A foreign owner gives a passport with a photo and a residence document or a work permit, and files at the registry for the place shown in the residence document. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

Can a foreigner buy a motorbike in Thailand?

The registration rule lists the motorcycle as vehicle type 12 next to cars, and its clause for foreign owners sits in the general rule for registering a vehicle. A registration comes with proof of compulsory insurance that has not run out. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

What papers does a used car need?

For a final sale the transfer needs the registration book, proof of the sale such as a receipt or a sale contract, and the ID of seller and buyer. For a hire-purchase car it needs the contract, the instalment receipts and the last tax invoice with a letter confirming full payment. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

How is a used motorcycle transferred?

Seller and buyer sign the transfer form at the registry and bring the motorcycle for inspection. The fees are ฿5 for the form and ฿100 for the transfer, and the service takes one working day. The transfer is notified within 15 days of the sale. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

What does a used motorcycle need each year?

The yearly tax on a private motorcycle is ฿100, paid with proof of compulsory insurance that is still in force. A motorcycle registered for 5 years or more passes an inspection at a private inspection station first. The tax can be paid up to 90 days ahead. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

How much is the yearly tax on a used car?

A private car of up to 7 seats pays ฿0.50 a cc for the first 600 cc, ฿1.50 a cc from 601 to 1,800 cc and ฿4 a cc above that. On those rates a 1,200 cc car pays ฿1,200 and a 2,000 cc car ฿2,900. From the sixth year of registration the tax falls by 10%, and by 50% in the tenth year. chaiyaphumdlt.go.th

Is there stamp duty on a used car sale?

The Revenue Department’s tariff for a receipt of sale or transfer of a registered vehicle is ฿1 for every ฿200 or part of ฿200 of the amount. A receipt for income that carries VAT or specific business tax is exempt. rd.go.th
