Good to know

  • Under section 38 of the Immigration Act, house owners, heads of household, landlords and hotel managers who accommodate a foreigner on a temporary basis notify immigration within 24 hours of the foreigner's arrival, on form TM.30. The notice goes in person, through an authorised person, by registered mail or over the internet. immigration.go.th
  • A foreigner who stays in Thailand more than 90 days reports their address every 90 days, from 15 days before to 7 days after the due date. The report is made online, in person, by registered post or by sending someone else with the notice. immigration.go.th
  • Online 90-day reports stop working after a passport change; the report is then made in person or by an authorised person. immigration.go.th
  • The Immigration Bureau's page lists a fine of ฿2,000 for a late 90-day report, which is then made in person. immigration.go.th

What people ask

Can I file the TM30 online?

Yes. The Immigration Bureau lists four ways to notify: in person, through an authorised person, by registered mail or via the internet. The internet route has its own list of papers on the same page. immigration.go.th

Who files the TM30, the landlord or the foreigner?

The page names house owners, heads of household, landlords and managers of hotels who accommodate foreign nationals on a temporary basis. The deadline is 24 hours from the foreigner's arrival. immigration.go.th

When is the 90-day report possible, and can someone else make it?

The report comes every 90 days, with a window from 15 days before to 7 days after the due date. The page lists an online route, going in person, registered mail, and sending another person with the notice. immigration.go.th

What changes after a passport change?

The online 90-day report is not supported after a passport change. The report is then made in person at the immigration office for the area where the foreigner lives, by the foreigner or a representative. Online reporting resumes at the next scheduled date. immigration.go.th
