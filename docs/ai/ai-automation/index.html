Good to know

  • n8n run on your own server, with no licence key added, is the free Community edition. docs.n8n.io
  • n8n's licence allows use for your own internal business purposes or for non-commercial or personal use. It allows passing the software to others only free of charge and for non-commercial purposes. github.com
  • Zapier's Free plan holds 100 tasks a month and only two-step Zaps: one trigger and one action. zapier.com
  • Zapier counts one task for each successful action step. Triggers, Filter, Paths, Formatter and Delay steps, and steps that fail, use none. help.zapier.com
  • Make's Free plan gives 1,000 credits a month and 1 MB of data storage in one data store. make.com
  • Cloudflare Workers Free allows 100,000 requests a day, with 10 ms of CPU time for each request. The paid plan costs US$5 a month and includes 10 million requests a month. developers.cloudflare.com

What people ask

Is n8n automation free or paid?

n8n Cloud, the hosted version, is paid. Starter is €20 a month billed yearly, with 2,500 runs a month, and Pro is €50 with 10,000. A trial allows 1,000 runs. A standard self-hosted version is also on GitHub. n8n.io

Is Zapier automation free?

Partly. The Free plan stops at 100 tasks a month and two-step Zaps. Professional costs US$19.99 a month billed yearly, or US$29.99 billed monthly, from 750 tasks. zapier.com

How do Zapier, Make, n8n and Cloudflare count what you use?

Zapier counts a task for each successful action. Make counts a credit for each module action. n8n counts one execution, one run of the whole workflow, whatever the number of steps. A Cloudflare Worker counts one request. hongdam.net
