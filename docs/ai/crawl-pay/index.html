Good to know

  • Cloudflare's pay per crawl is in closed beta. The page points to a signup page, and Enterprise customers can contact their account executive. developers.cloudflare.com
  • A crawler that does not pay receives an HTTP 402 Payment Required answer that includes the price. A crawler that sends its payment intent in the request headers gets a normal answer, HTTP 200. developers.cloudflare.com
  • The site owner sets the price. The Cloudflare post describes a flat price per request across the whole site, and the owner can let chosen crawlers in free of charge. blog.cloudflare.com
  • Cloudflare acts as the Merchant of Record for pay per crawl. A WAF or Bot Management rule that blocks a crawler overrides the charge, so that crawler gets no access. developers.cloudflare.com
  • Cloudflare's AI Crawl Control shows which AI services access a site, sets allow or block rules for individual crawlers, and tracks robots.txt compliance. It works on all Cloudflare plans. developers.cloudflare.com

What people ask

How much does Cloudflare pay per crawl cost?

The introduction post gives no price and shows only a placeholder, USD XX.XX. The site owner sets the price per request. blog.cloudflare.com

Can anyone use pay per crawl?

Not yet. Cloudflare describes it as a closed beta. Site owners and crawler operators sign up through Cloudflare's pay per crawl signup page, and Enterprise customers can contact their account executive. developers.cloudflare.com

What choices does a site owner have for each bot?

Three. Allow gives free access. Charge asks for the price set for the whole domain, and works even for a crawler that has no billing relationship with Cloudflare yet. Block denies access, with no way to pay. blog.cloudflare.com

How can I see which bots follow my robots.txt?

Cloudflare's AI Crawl Control has a dashboard of crawler activity and monitors robots.txt compliance. It shows which crawlers follow the directives, and rules can be created to enforce them. The page says it works on all Cloudflare plans. developers.cloudflare.com
