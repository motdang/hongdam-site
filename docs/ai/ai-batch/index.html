What people ask

How long does a bulk AI job take?

Anthropic says most batches finish in less than 1 hour. Results are available when all requests are done or after 24 hours, whichever comes first, and a batch that is not done in 24 hours expires. platform.claude.com

How does OpenAI price a bulk job?

OpenAI lists a 50% discount compared with its synchronous API. Each batch completes within 24 hours, and often sooner. A batch file holds up to 50,000 requests and 200 MB. developers.openai.com

How big can a bulk job be?

At Anthropic, a batch is limited to 100,000 requests or 256 MB, whichever is reached first. At OpenAI the limit is 50,000 requests and 200 MB for the input file. platform.claude.com

How long is the data kept?

Anthropic stores a batch's requests and results for up to 29 days after the batch is created. A batch can be deleted at any time after processing, and an unfinished batch is cancelled first. platform.claude.com

What does data cleaning cover?

One Fastwork seller lists duplicates removed, missing values handled, and dates, numbers, phone numbers and categories made uniform. The same listing covers merging several files and a report that compares the data before and after. fastwork.co
