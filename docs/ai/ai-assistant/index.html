What people ask

What does a chatbot tool cost a month?

Tidio's Lyro AI agent starts at US$32.50 a month for 50 Lyro conversations. Tidio's Starter plan, for 100 billable conversations, is US$24.17 a month. Its Free plan costs US$0. tidio.com

How much does it cost to have an AI chatbot made in Thailand?

One Fastwork seller offers a ChatGPT chatbot for LINE, Facebook Messenger and Instagram for ฿4,800. It takes 3 days, includes 1 revision and has sold 29 times. fastwork.co

Can a chatbot answer on LINE?

Yes. Fastwork sellers build chatbots for a LINE Official Account. In one listing the Starter package, ฿15,000 in 5 days, answers questions about FAQs, menus and prices and passes the chat to a person when the bot cannot answer. fastwork.co

How much does one answer cost in model fees?

Take 3,000 tokens in and 300 tokens out for one answer. Claude Haiku 5.5 charges US$0.00045 for that, and Claude Sonnet 5.5 charges US$0.009. At ฿33.71 to the dollar, that is ฿0.015 and ฿0.30. platform.claude.com

Does any model run free?

Cloudflare Workers AI gives 10,000 Neurons a day at no charge. Above that, it charges US$0.011 per 1,000 Neurons on the Workers Paid plan. developers.cloudflare.com

What does a white-label chatbot cost on other tools?

White-label means the tool shows your name, not the maker's. Chatbase charges US$99 a month to remove its "Powered By Chatbase" label on its Hobby, Basic and Business plans. It lists white-labelling as an Enterprise feature. chatbase.co

How does Zendesk charge for AI answers?

Zendesk charges US$1.50 for each automated resolution on a committed plan and US$2.00 pay as you go. Its Suite Team plan costs US$55 per agent a month, billed yearly. zendesk.com

What does a person cost to answer messages?

An OnlineJobs.ph guide lists customer-service assistants in the Philippines at US$3–5 an hour at the start, US$4–7 with 1 to 5 years of experience and US$6–8 with more than 5 years. Monthly, that is US$400–600, US$460–750 and US$700–900. blog.onlinejobs.ph
