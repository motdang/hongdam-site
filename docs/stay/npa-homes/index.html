What people ask

What is an NPA property?

NPA stands for non-performing asset. It is a property a lender fully owns. The lender took it from a borrower who did not pay, received it by agreement in place of the debt, or bought it back at a Legal Execution Department auction. KTC's article lists these three routes. ktc.co.th

Can a foreigner buy a condo that a bank sells?

A foreigner may own condo units freehold up to 49% of a building's floor area. The rule for land is in the facts above. thethaiger.com

What does the Land Office charge on a transfer?

The Department of Lands' fee page gives a registration fee of 2% of the assessed value for land and for condos. Stamp duty is ฿1 for each ฿200, which is 0.5%. Specific business tax is 3.3% and applies to some sales, such as a sale within 5 years of acquiring the property. dol.go.th

Does the buyer pay the bank's taxes?

KBank's questions page lists the bank as the payer of its corporate income tax and its specific business tax. The buyer pays the transfer fee. A buyer with a KBank loan also pays the mortgage registration fee, the stamp duty on the loan and fire insurance. The valuation fee is waived for a KBank loan. support.linebk.com

What if someone lives in the property?

KBank's questions page marks such a property as one with a user. Once the bank owns it, the bank negotiates or files an eviction suit. If a suit is open and the bank sells the property as it stands, the bank ends the suit and the buyer carries on the eviction. support.linebk.com
