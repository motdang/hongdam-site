Useful facts

  • The Tourism Authority of Thailand lists three licences a foreigner can drive on: one issued under Thai law, an International Driving Permit, or a licence from an ASEAN member country shown with the domestic licence. It says that to rent a car or motorbike, you present a valid driving licence or permit. tatnews.org
  • TAT's page says motorbike riders and passengers must wear helmets. It also says that without proper documents for a rental vehicle, insurance cover may be void after an accident. tatnews.org
  • One Fastwork seller's Chiang Mai airport car rental holds ฿6,000 on a physical credit card in the driver's name (฿8,000 for an electric car). It takes no debit cards, cash or digital wallets. Drivers are 21 to 65 and have held a licence for at least 1 year. fastwork.co
  • A Chiang Mai scooter guide says reputable shops usually include a helmet, and that a car licence from home may not cover the scooter category. monthlychiangmai.pages.dev
  • Under Revenue Department Order Tor Por 4/2528 (English translation), a company or other juristic person that pays rent withholds income tax of 5% when the payee is an individual or a company doing business in Thailand, and 10% when the payee is a foundation or association that earns revenue. rd.go.th

What people ask

How much does a scooter rental cost in Chiang Mai?

A Chiang Mai rental guide gives ฿150–350 a day for a 110–125cc scooter and ฿3,000–4,500 a month. The usual cash deposit is ฿2,000–5,000, and up to ฿10,000 for a premium bike. monthlychiangmai.pages.dev

Can I rent a scooter by the month in Chiang Mai?

Yes. The same guide lists ฿3,000–4,500 a month for 110–125cc scooters and up to about ฿6,600 for bigger or newer bikes. monthlychiangmai.pages.dev

Can I rent a car in Chiang Mai without a credit card?

Terms depend on the shop. One airport rental listing on Fastwork asks for a physical credit card in the driver's name for a ฿6,000 hold. It does not take debit cards, cash or digital wallets. fastwork.co

What licence does a visitor need to rent a car or scooter?

The Tourism Authority of Thailand lists a licence issued under Thai law, an International Driving Permit, or an ASEAN member country's licence shown with the domestic licence. To rent a car or motorbike, a valid licence or permit is presented. tatnews.org

Does a company withhold tax when it pays rent?

Yes. The Revenue Department's order sets 5% when the payee is an individual or a company doing business in Thailand, and 10% for a foundation or association that earns revenue. rd.go.th
