Useful facts

  • A hotel booking engine collects the reservation and card details from the guest and passes them to the hotel's systems, such as its property management system. Its booking flow sits on the hotel's own domain and not on a separate hosted page. cloudbeds.com
  • Cloudbeds puts the commission of online travel agencies at 15% to 30% or more of each booking. A decade ago the average sat near 10%. Niche and regional agencies charge as little as 4%. cloudbeds.com
  • Cloudbeds reports that 21.8% of online-agency bookings were cancelled in 2025, against 10.6% of direct bookings. cloudbeds.com
  • Booking.com sets its commission as a percentage in the property's Accommodation Agreement. It can apply to nights, room price, rooms booked and eligible extras, and charged cancellations and no-shows can attract it too. smartorder.ai
  • Agoda's compensation is either a percentage of the reference sell rate or a markup on the loaded rate. The property pays it, on conditions such as a confirmed stay. A property finds its rate in the Partner Portal under Finance, Contracted compensation. partnerhub.agoda.com
  • In the unofficial English translation of the Hotel Act B.E. 2547 on the Department of Provincial Administration's site, no person operates a hotel business without a licence from the Registrar (section 15). A licence covers the hotel named in it and is valid for five years (section 19). The hotel's name shows in Thai letters, and foreign letters may follow it or sit below it (section 20). multi.dopa.go.th

What people ask

What is a hotel booking engine?

A booking engine is software that runs on the hotel's own website. Guests see current vacancies and rates for each room category, then reserve and pay, from any device, in a two-step booking process. cloudbeds.com

How much does a booking engine cost?

Beds24's price page starts at €15.50 a month, scaled to the number of rooms or units, and adds €0.55 a month for each channel link. It lists online bookings without commission as included at no extra charge. A private-label subdomain such as bookings.mywebsite.com costs €19 a month. beds24.com

How much does a hotel website cost?

One Fastwork listing prices a WordPress hotel website at ฿3,500 with no booking system, ฿6,500 with an online booking system and live room availability, and ฿12,000 with a booking dashboard and 30 days of support. Delivery takes 4, 6 and 7 days. The packages list a domain and hosting for 1 year. fastwork.co

How much do booking sites take from a booking?

SmartOrder's guide puts Booking.com at about 10% to 20%, Agoda at about 15% to 25% and Expedia at about 15% to 30%, as industry estimates. The guide says Expedia's final rate varies by market, property, payment model and negotiated terms, and Booking.com's percentage is set in the Accommodation Agreement. smartorder.ai

Does Agoda's commission depend on where the hotel is?

Yes. Agoda's Partner Hub says compensation depends on the property's location and, in some cases, the payment model. It says rates reflect local marketing prices and expenses, so they cannot be standardized across locations. partnerhub.agoda.com

What does it cost to take PromptPay and card payments on a website?

Omise's Thailand price list shows PromptPay QR at 1.65% a payment, cards at 3.65% and mobile banking at ฿10, before 7% VAT. A transfer from the Omise account to a bank account costs ฿20 up to ฿2,000,000. omise.co

Which stays count as a hotel under Thai law?

The Hotel Act B.E. 2547 defines a hotel as accommodation set up as a business to give travellers or others temporary lodging for payment. It leaves out lodging run by a state body, or for charity or education, not on a profit or income-sharing basis, lodging offered only at a monthly charge or upward, and other kinds a Ministerial Regulation names. multi.dopa.go.th
