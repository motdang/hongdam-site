What people ask

Who pays the agent when a home is sold in Thailand?

The seller. RE/MAX Thailand's page says the seller is almost exclusively responsible for the commission, and that a buyer pays nothing unless the buyer signed a buyer's agency agreement. remaxthailand.prod.cms.realestateplatform.com

How much commission does an agent take on a rental?

PropertyScout says its commission is paid by the landlord, usually one monthly rent, and that the service is free for tenants and buyers. propertyscout.co.th

When is the sale commission paid?

At the transfer at the Land Office, usually by cashier's cheque or bank transfer when the seller receives the proceeds. RE/MAX Thailand's page says it is paid to the brokerage company, not the individual agent, for a proper tax invoice. remaxthailand.prod.cms.realestateplatform.com

Does the commission carry VAT?

Yes. RE/MAX Thailand's page says brokerage services carry 7% VAT, on the commission and not on the property price. It gives the example of a ฿10,000,000 sale at 3%: ฿300,000 commission, ฿21,000 VAT. remaxthailand.prod.cms.realestateplatform.com

Who reports a foreign tenant to Immigration?

The house owner, head of household, landlord or hotel manager, under section 38 of the 1979 Immigration Act. The report is on form TM.30, within 24 hours of the foreign person's arrival, at the local immigration office or, where there is none, the local police station. immigration.go.th
