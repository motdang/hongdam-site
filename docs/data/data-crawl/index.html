Good to know

  • A robots.txt file tells search engine crawlers which URLs they can access on a site. Google's page says it does not keep a page out of Google, and that it is up to the crawler to obey it. developers.google.com
  • RFC 9309, the standard for robots.txt, says its rules are not a form of access authorization. Crawlers are asked to follow them. rfc-editor.org
  • Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) covers information that identifies a natural person (Section 6). If a business collects it from a source other than the person, Section 25 asks for notice within 30 days and consent, unless an exception applies. cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • Google Maps Platform's terms (3.2.3) say a customer will not scrape Google Maps content for use outside the services, including copying and saving business names, addresses or user reviews. cloud.google.com

What people ask

Can a website tell a crawler to stay away?

Yes. A site publishes a robots.txt file that lists the URLs a crawler may open. Google's page says it is up to the crawler to obey the file. Google also says the file does not keep a page out of Google. developers.google.com

What is the fine under Thailand's data protection law?

Section 83 sets an administrative fine of up to ฿3 million for breaking Section 25 paragraph one, which covers collecting personal data from a source other than the person. Section 84 sets up to ฿5 million for collecting data such as health, religion or biometric data without explicit consent (Section 26). cc.kmutt.ac.th

Can a crawler copy business names from Google Maps?

Google Maps Platform's terms say no. Section 3.2.3 lists copying and saving business names, addresses or user reviews among the things a customer will not do with Google Maps content. cloud.google.com
