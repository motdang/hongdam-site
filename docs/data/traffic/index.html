What people ask

What is a verified bot?

Cloudflare calls a verified bot a bot or agent that it has confirmed is open about who it is and what it does. Examples are search engine crawlers, monitoring services and user-driven agents. developers.cloudflare.com

Does Google Analytics count bots?

Google Analytics excludes traffic from known bots and spiders automatically. Google's page says the exclusion cannot be turned off and that the amount of excluded bot traffic cannot be seen. support.google.com

Can I count visits on a site that is not on Cloudflare?

Yes. Cloudflare's set-up page for Web Analytics adds the site by hostname, then gives a JavaScript snippet to put in the site's HTML pages before the closing body tag. developers.cloudflare.com
