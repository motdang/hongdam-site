Good to know

  • If a business collects personal data from a source other than the person, Section 25 of Thailand's PDPA asks for notice within 30 days and the person's consent, unless an exception applies. cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • Under Section 32, a person may object at any time to their data being collected, used or disclosed for direct marketing. After an objection, the business can no longer collect, use or disclose that data. cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • Section 83 sets an administrative fine of up to ฿3 million for breaking Section 25 paragraph one or Section 32 paragraph two. cc.kmutt.ac.th

Who does Thailand's data protection law protect?

Natural persons. Section 6 defines personal data as information that identifies a natural person, directly or indirectly. It leaves out information of the deceased. cc.kmutt.ac.th

Where can I look up a Thai company?

The Department of Business Development runs DBD DataWarehouse+. Its menu lists juristic person data and financial statements, business types, foreign investment in Thai companies, business partners and statistics. The page names dbd.go.th and datawarehouse.dbd.go.th as the official addresses. datawarehouse.dbd.go.th
