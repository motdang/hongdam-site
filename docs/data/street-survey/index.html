Good to know

  • Thailand charges a signboard tax by the script on the sign. A report on the rates from 1 January 2021 gives ฿5 per 500 square centimetres for Thai letters only, ฿26 for Thai mixed with foreign letters or pictures, and ฿50 for no Thai letters or Thai placed below foreign letters. nationthailand.com
  • The Department of Business Development's DataWarehouse+ site is a search service for registered companies and other juristic persons (legal entities). It lists company information and financial statements, business types and area-based data. The site is in Thai. datawarehouse.dbd.go.th
