Good to know

  • WordPress's documentation says a backup has two parts, the database and the files, and both are needed to restore a typical WordPress site fully. developer.wordpress.org
  • The same page gives these guidelines: back up the database regularly and before an upgrade; once a week for smaller websites with fewer posts; daily for high-activity websites with a lot of posts. developer.wordpress.org
  • The CA/Browser Forum's ballot SC-081v3 sets out a cut in the maximum life of public TLS certificates (the certificates behind https) from 398 days to 47 days, starting in March 2026 and concluding in March 2029. cabforum.org
  • A Bangkok agency's managed hosting and maintenance page lists regular updates, daily backups stored off-site, uptime monitoring, malware scans, spam filters, brute-force protection and a support team. It gives no price. asiamediastudio.com
