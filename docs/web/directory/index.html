What people ask

How do I make a business directory website?

On WordPress, a directory plugin is one way. Business Directory Plugin has a free version on WordPress.org and paid plans from US$99 for the first year. businessdirectoryplugin.com

Can I copy business details from Google Maps?

Google Maps Platform's terms bar copying business names, addresses or reviews out of Maps. cloud.google.com

What does Google need to show a business in search?

Google's LocalBusiness structured data requires the business name and address. Geo coordinates, opening hours, telephone and URL are recommended, and the page shows its examples in JSON-LD. developers.google.com

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a proposal for a Markdown file at the site root, or any path in it, that guides AI agents through a site. It has a required H1 with the site name, an optional summary, and sections of link lists. llmstxt.org
