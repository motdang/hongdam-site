Useful facts

  • A Progressive Web App is a web app that a supporting browser offers to install. Once installed, it gets an app icon on the device and can open as a standalone app, without browser buttons. It needs a web app manifest and an https address. developer.mozilla.org
  • A service worker lets a web app work without a connection. When it installs, it fetches pages, styles, scripts and images and adds them to a local cache. MDN says that in many situations the web app then works well even when the connection comes and goes. developer.mozilla.org
  • StatCounter puts Android at 73.96% and iOS at 26.01% of mobile operating systems in Thailand for September 2026. gs.statcounter.com
  • Thailand falls under the remaining markets on Google Play's fee page. There the service fee is 15% on the first US$1 million of a developer's revenue a year, and a higher rate above that. Auto-renewing subscriptions are 15%. support.google.com
  • Apple's Small Business Program lowers its commission to 15% on paid apps and in-app purchases. Developers who made up to US$1 million in proceeds the year before, and developers new to the App Store, can qualify. developer.apple.com
  • From 30 September 2026, Google's developer verification protections begin for users in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand who install apps from participating stores on certified devices running Android 7 or later. Developers who distribute only outside Google Play register in the Android Developer Console. developer.android.com

What is a Progressive Web App?

It is a web app that a supporting browser offers to install. Once installed, the app gets an icon on the device and can open as a standalone app, with no browser buttons. It needs a web app manifest and an https address. developer.mozilla.org

Can an app work with no signal?

A web app works without a connection when it has a service worker. When the service worker installs, it fetches pages, styles, scripts and images and adds them to a local cache. MDN says that in many situations the web app then works well even when the connection comes and goes. developer.mozilla.org

What does it cost to register as a developer on Google Play?

Google charges a one-time registration fee of US$25, payable by credit or debit card. If the identity information is found invalid, the fee is not refunded. support.google.com

What does Google Play keep from sales?

For the remaining markets, which include Thailand, Google Play's page lists 15% on the first US$1 million of a developer's revenue a year, and a higher rate above that. Auto-renewing subscriptions are 15%. support.google.com

What does an Apple developer account cost?

Apple's Developer Program page lists a US$99 annual membership. The page shows the price in US dollars only. developer.apple.com

Where do developers register an Android app that is not on Google Play?

Google's developer verification page lists the Android Developer Console for developers who distribute apps only outside Google Play. A limited distribution account lets students, teachers and hobbyists share an app with up to 20 devices without a government ID or registration fee. developer.android.com
