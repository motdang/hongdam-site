Good to know

  • LY Corporation, the company behind LINE, counts 54 million monthly active users of LINE in Thailand as of 31 March 2026. It puts this at 81.8% of the population, using a 2024 population of about 65.95 million from the Ministry of Interior. lycorp.co.jp
  • A LINE MINI App is a web application that runs on LINE. People open it from inside LINE, a permanent link, a LINE Official Account, the Home tab, search or a shared message. developers.line.biz
  • A new LINE MINI App channel starts as an unverified MINI App, with some features restricted. For a service in Thailand, only channels under a certified provider can apply for the verification review. developers.line.biz
  • LINE counts messages as the number of requests multiplied by the number of recipients. Reply messages are not counted toward a plan's message count, and messages to users who blocked the account are not counted either. developers.line.biz
  • The Messaging API documentation says that past the monthly message limit, an error response is returned and the messages are not sent. developers.line.biz
  • A LINE Official Account that passes LINE's review gets a Verified account badge. LINE accepts review applications in Japan, Taiwan and Thailand and issues no verified accounts in other countries or regions at this time. help.line.me

What people ask

What is a LINE Official Account?

LINE Thailand describes it as a business's official account on LINE. A shop uses it to build a follower base and send customers news of sales and promotions. Its features include a greeting message, auto-reply, coupons and point cards, one-to-one chat and broadcasts. lineforbusiness.com

Does it cost money to make a LINE OA?

Opening an official account gives 300 free messages a month. LINE Thailand sells two monthly plans on top: Basic at ฿1,280 for 15,000 messages and Pro at ฿1,780 for 35,000. Both prices exclude 7% VAT. lineforbusiness.com

How do I log in to a LINE OA?

On a computer, LINE Official Account Manager is at manager.line.biz. On a phone, the Official Account app is the way in. Both log in with a LINE account or a business account. lineforbusiness.com

What is the difference between a LINE MINI App and LIFF?

LY Corporation says LIFF will eventually be brought into the LINE MINI App brand, and recommends creating new LIFF apps as LINE MINI Apps. Some LIFF features are missing from a MINI App: the action button cannot be hidden, Module Mode cannot be set, a channel holds one web app, and the screen size is fixed to Full. developers.line.biz

Who builds a LINE MINI App?

A developer. LINE Developers says a LINE MINI App channel is created with a LINE Developers Console account. The app is a web application that runs on LINE. developers.line.biz

Can a LINE OA run a shop?

LINE Thailand lists MyShop next to the plans as a tool that builds a storefront for a LINE OA and helps close sales. Orders completed through MyShop carry a fee under conditions LINE sets. lineforbusiness.com

Can customers book and pay a deposit inside LINE?

Fastwork sellers list booking through a LINE OA. In one listing, the starter package takes bookings and sends booking notices to the LINE OA. The next adds a deposit with a slip upload and a fake-slip check. The top one adds reminders before the appointment, rescheduling and cancelling. fastwork.co
