Good to know

  • Google Workspace's mail records use the MX value smtp.google.com at priority 1 (an MX record is the line in a domain's settings that says where its mail goes). Google's page says new MX records can take up to 72 hours to be recognised, and lists removing any other MX records as a step. knowledge.workspace.google.com
  • Microsoft 365 needs an MX record for the mail of a custom domain. An Autodiscover CNAME record and an SPF TXT record are optional, and Microsoft's page calls them highly recommended. When the MX record changes, all new mail for the domain goes to Microsoft 365, and existing mail stays at the previous host unless it is moved. learn.microsoft.com
  • For all senders, Gmail requires SPF or DKIM. Senders of more than 5,000 messages a day to Gmail accounts need SPF, DKIM and DMARC (three records that show a message comes from the domain it names). The DMARC policy can be set to none. support.google.com
  • Cloudflare Email Routing takes mail that arrives at an address on a domain and passes it to another destination. It does not send mail; sending is a separate Cloudflare product. developers.cloudflare.com

What people ask

How much does Google Workspace cost in Thailand?

Google's Thai pricing page lists Starter at ฿275, Standard at ฿550 and Plus at ฿850 a user a month, before tax. For new customers it shows ฿137.50 for Starter and ฿330 for Standard, for the first 20 users and the first 12 months. workspace.google.com

Is there a free way to get email at my own domain?

Cloudflare Email Routing is offered on Cloudflare's Free plan. It passes mail that arrives at a domain address to another destination, and it does not send mail. Sending is a separate product, in beta on the Workers Paid plan. developers.cloudflare.com

How long until mail arrives after the MX records change?

Google's page says it can take up to 72 hours for new MX records to be recognised. It adds that mail may not flow until the Gmail activation step in the Admin console is done too. knowledge.workspace.google.com

Which records does Gmail ask a sending domain for?

Gmail asks all senders for SPF or DKIM. Senders of more than 5,000 messages a day to Gmail accounts need SPF, DKIM and DMARC. The same page names a spam rate below 0.3% in Postmaster Tools. support.google.com

Can Outlook and Microsoft 365 be used for mail on my own domain?

Yes. Microsoft's page explains how to connect a domain bought elsewhere by adding DNS records at the registrar, and the domain stays registered there. Without a custom domain, an onmicrosoft.com address is used. learn.microsoft.com
