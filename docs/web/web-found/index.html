Good to know

  • Google's page on AI features says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. It says a site does not need to create new machine-readable files, AI text files or markup. To appear as a supporting link, a page must be indexed (stored by Google) and eligible to show in Search with a snippet. developers.google.com
  • Google's page on title links says Google builds the title of a search result from several sources. They include the title element of the page, its main visible title, headings such as h1 and og:title tags. The page says a title should be descriptive and concise, and different from page to page. Google may write a new title when one is half-empty, out of date, inaccurate or repeated on many pages. developers.google.com
  • Google defines a sitemap as a file with information about the pages, videos and other files on a site. It says a sitemap does not guarantee that the items in it are crawled (visited by Google's program) and indexed. A site of about 500 pages or fewer that is well linked inside counts as small, and Google says such a site may not need one. developers.google.com
  • A robots.txt file is a text file that tells crawlers, the programs that visit pages, which URLs they can access on a site. Google says it is used mainly to avoid overloading a site with requests and is not a way to keep a page out of Google. A page blocked in robots.txt can still be indexed if other sites link to it. developers.google.com
  • OpenAI lists three crawlers. OAI-SearchBot is the name for managing how a site appears in ChatGPT search. GPTBot collects content that may be used to train its models. ChatGPT-User visits a page when a user asks, and OpenAI says robots.txt rules may not apply to it. A robots.txt change can take about 24 hours to reach OpenAI's search systems. developers.openai.com
  • Anthropic lists three bots. ClaudeBot collects web content that may contribute to model training. Claude-User fetches pages when a Claude user asks a question. Claude-SearchBot analyses content to improve search results. Anthropic says its bots follow robots.txt and support the non-standard Crawl-delay extension. support.claude.com
  • The llms.txt page calls it a proposal to standardise a file named llms.txt that helps AI agents use a website. The file is written in Markdown, a plain-text format with simple marks, and sits at the site root or at a path within it. Only the first-level heading with the site name is required. A short summary and lists of links are optional parts. llmstxt.org

What people ask

How much does SEO cost in Thailand?

A Bangkok agency, Relevant Audience, says SEO in Thailand generally runs about ฿20,000 to ฿150,000 or more a month. It gives ฿40,000 to ฿60,000 a month as the usual start for a fully managed campaign. Its page lists technical SEO, content, link building, local SEO and monthly reports as parts of the service. relevantaudience.com

What does SEO cost on Fastwork?

One Fastwork seller lists SEO and AI-ranking work built on backlinks, which are links to a site from other sites. Package B costs ฿3,000 for 500–700 backlinks and a report of the links placed. The package card says 7 days. The listing shows 1.6K sales, a 4.9 rating and a 100% completion rate. Only package B shows a price on the page. fastwork.co

Does it cost money to appear in Google's results?

Google's page on hiring an SEO says appearing in Google's organic search results is free. It also says Google does not accept money to include or rank sites in those results. developers.google.com

Can anyone guarantee first place on Google?

Google states that no one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google. It describes an SEO audit as about giving estimates of improvement that match what can be done. developers.google.com

How many searchers click the first Google result?

In Backlinko's study the first Google result has a click-through rate of 27.6%. The top three results get 54.4% of all clicks. Only 0.63% of searchers click something on the second page. Backlinko's page was last updated on 16 April 2025. backlinko.com

Does a site need an AI text file to appear in Google's AI answers?

Google's page on AI features says a site does not need to create new machine-readable files, AI text files or markup to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible to show in Search with a snippet. The same page names robots.txt rules for Googlebot as the way site owners control access. developers.google.com

Does a small site need a sitemap?

Google says a site of about 500 pages or fewer that is well linked inside may not need one. A new site with few links from other sites can need one, because Googlebot may not find pages that no other site links to. Google also says a sitemap does not guarantee that its items are crawled and indexed. developers.google.com

Does Google read the phone version or the desktop version of a page?

Google says it uses the mobile version of a site's content, crawled with the smartphone agent, for indexing and ranking. Only the content shown on the mobile site is used for indexing. Google recommends responsive web design, where the content and the metadata are the same on phone and desktop. developers.google.com
