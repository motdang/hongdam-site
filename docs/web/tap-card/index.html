What people ask

Is there a free digital business card?

HiHello's Personal plan is free and includes 2 digital business cards. They can be shared by QR, widget, email, SMS and WhatsApp, and added to Apple and Google Wallet. hihello.com

Does a QR code cost a licence fee?

No. DENSO WAVE's FAQ answers that no licence is necessary for a QR Code that follows the JIS or ISO standards, and that commercial use needs no licensing contract. qrcode.com

How does someone open my card from the QR code?

On an iPhone, Apple's support article says to open the Camera app or Code Scanner and hold the device so the QR code appears. Then the person taps the link to open the content. support.apple.com

What is a vCard?

vCard is the data format defined in RFC 6350 for representing and exchanging information about individuals. It covers name, delivery address, email address, multiple telephone numbers, photograph, logo and audio clips. rfc-editor.org
