Useful facts

  • Cloudflare can scan for a domain's existing DNS records and add them, but its page says the scan is not guaranteed to find them all, and the records for the domain itself, www and email need a review. developers.cloudflare.com
  • Cloudflare's page says to wait up to 24 hours while the registrar updates the nameservers. developers.cloudflare.com
  • Cloudflare says DNSSEC is turned off at the registrar before the nameservers change, because changing them while it is on can make the domain unreachable. developers.cloudflare.com
  • On every Cloudflare plan, a CNAME record on the domain itself (yourshop.com, without www) is flattened by default, so it works where many other DNS hosts refuse it. developers.cloudflare.com
  • Cloudflare Email Routing, on the Free plan, sends incoming mail for addresses on the domain to an inbox you already use. It handles incoming mail only. developers.cloudflare.com

What people ask

Will my email stop working?

Cloudflare says its scan may miss records and that mail values depend on your email provider. Our check compares every email record on the old and new settings before the switch, and again after. developers.cloudflare.com

How long does the switch take?

Cloudflare's page says up to 24 hours while the registrar updates the nameservers. developers.cloudflare.com

Does the domain itself move?

No. The domain stays with your registrar; only its nameservers change there. Cloudflare Registrar can take the domain too, at the registry's price. developers.cloudflare.com

Does Cloudflare charge for this?

The Free plan has no charge and includes free SSL certificates. cloudflare.com
