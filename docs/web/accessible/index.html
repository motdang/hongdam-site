Good to know

  • WCAG 2.2, the W3C's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, is the latest version. It was published on 5 October 2023 and updated on 12 December 2024. Its testable rules sit at three levels: A, AA and AAA. WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 remain valid standards. w3.org
  • WCAG asks for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 between text and its background, and 3:1 for large text. Large text is 18 point, or 14 point bold, which is about 24 and 18.5 pixels. w3.org
  • WCAG asks that text can be resized up to 200 percent without assistive technology and without loss of content or functionality. Browser zoom counts as one way to do it. w3.org
  • WCAG asks that images and other non-text content have a text alternative that serves the same purpose. Pure decoration is hidden from assistive technology, because alternative text on it distracts people who use screen readers. w3.org
  • Lighthouse scores accessibility as a weighted average of pass-or-fail audits such as button and link names, ARIA attributes, color contrast, image alt text, form labels, heading order and the page language. Manual audits are not part of the score. developer.chrome.com

What people ask

What is an accessibility audit?

The W3C describes a first review called Easy Checks. It covers the page title, text alternatives for images, headings, contrast, text resizing, keyboard access, forms and moving content. The page says a page could pass these checks and still have significant barriers, and it points to the WCAG-EM method and to involving users for a thorough evaluation. w3.org

How much does a website accessibility audit cost?

Accessible.org's pricing page gives US$1,250 to US$2,750 for most website audits. It prices a primary page or screen at US$100 to US$250 and a light one at US$25 to US$100. accessible.org

Is there a free accessibility checker?

TestParty's cost guide lists WAVE, axe DevTools and Lighthouse as automated scans that cost US$0. testparty.ai

What contrast does WCAG require?

At least 4.5:1 between text and its background, and 3:1 for large text. Large text is 18 point, or 14 point bold, which is about 24 and 18.5 pixels. w3.org

What does a WCAG audit report contain?

Accessible.org's Technical Audit Report is an Excel file with detailed findings. Its FAQ says it lists the WCAG criteria affected and the information needed to act on each issue. accessible.org
