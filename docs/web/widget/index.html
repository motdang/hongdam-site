What people ask

Is there a free weather widget for a website?

Open-Meteo's weather API is free for non-commercial use, up to 10,000 calls a day. Its paid plans cover commercial use, from 1 million calls a month. open-meteo.com

Can a box show PM2.5 for my town?

Open-Meteo lists PM2.5 among its hourly air-quality variables, with a US AQI and a European AQI. Its global forecast comes on a grid of 0.4°, about 45 km. open-meteo.com

Can a box find the visitor's location by itself?

A page can read a visitor's position only in a secure context, which means HTTPS. The browser asks the visitor for permission first. developer.mozilla.org

What does PM2.5 mean?

PM2.5 is fine particles in the air, 2.5 micrometres or less across. WHO says they penetrate the lungs and can enter the bloodstream. who.int
