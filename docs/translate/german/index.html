What people ask

What is Swiss German spelling?

It is German written without the ß: a double s stands in its place, as in Strasse. The Swiss Federal Chancellery's guide says the ß has been going from Swiss writing since the 1930s. bk.admin.ch

Can I use German text from Germany on a Swiss page?

The Federal Chancellery's guide, written for federal texts, says text from another German-speaking country has to be adapted to Swiss spelling, and a plain copy and paste does not do. It adds that the ß can be replaced with ss by Find and Replace. bk.admin.ch

How do I mark a page as Swiss German for Google?

Google's guide to localised pages shows hreflang="de-CH" for a Swiss German version. It also says Google does not use hreflang or the HTML lang attribute to detect the language of a page; it uses its own algorithms for that. developers.google.com
