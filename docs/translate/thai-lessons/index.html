What people ask

How much do Thai lessons cost in Chiang Mai?

TSL Chiang Mai lists private Thai lessons at ฿500 an hour at the school and ฿450 an hour online, bought as 30 hours, and a group course of 24 hours a month at ฿3,600. tslchiangmai.com

Can I get a visa to study Thai?

The Royal Thai Embassy in London lists a short course in Thai or English among the studies the ED visa covers. It asks for a letter from a Ministry of Education authority and a signed enrolment letter, and the visa is for a single entry. london.thaiembassy.org

Can I learn Thai online?

Yes. TSL Chiang Mai sells private Thai lessons online, 30 hours for ฿13,500, which is ฿450 an hour. tslchiangmai.com

Are there short university Thai courses in Chiang Mai?

Chiang Mai Rajabhat University lists Thai for Foreigners 1: 45 hours at ฿3,000 a person. Its table shows a minimum of 10 students. inter.cmru.ac.th
