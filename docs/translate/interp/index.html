What people ask

How much does an interpreter cost in Thailand?

A Fastwork seller lists online interpreting at ฿1,000 an hour, ฿800 an hour for several hours, ฿2,500 up to 4 hours and ฿5,000 up to 8 hours. The same seller takes in-person work only in Bangkok and the nearby provinces. fastwork.co

Does the police provide an interpreter?

In a criminal case, Section 13 of the Criminal Procedure Code says the inquiry official, public prosecutor or court provides an interpreter without delay when the injured person, the alleged offender, the defendant or a witness cannot speak or understand Thai, and the authority pays the interpreter's allowance and expenses. The site marks its English text as not yet verified; the Thai original governs. thailawonline.com

Can an interpreter work by phone or video?

Yes. A Fastwork seller sells online interpreting by the hour, for a half day of up to 4 hours and for a full day of up to 8 hours, in Thai, English, Chinese and Japanese. fastwork.co

Do hospitals in Chiang Rai have interpreters?

Bangkok Hospital Chiang Rai lists help in Chinese and Myanmar, and phone support in 24 other languages around the clock. Its customer service line is +66 (0) 52-051-990. bangkokhospital.com
