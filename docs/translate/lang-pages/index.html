What people ask

How much does proofreading cost in Thailand?

A Fastwork seller proofs Thai text at ฿10 for an A4 page in font size 16 and ฿5 for an A5 page. The check covers misspelt words, spacing and marks. fastwork.co

How do I mark a page as Thai or English?

The W3C says to put a language attribute on the html tag, as in <html lang="en"> for English. Other elements inherit it, and content in another language gets its own attribute. Subtags come from the IANA Language Subtag Registry. w3.org

Does Google read the lang attribute of a page?

Google says it does not use hreflang or the HTML lang attribute to detect the language of a page. It uses algorithms for that, and it uses hreflang to match a page to its localized versions. developers.google.com

Does a proofread rewrite my text?

An English proofreader on Fastwork states that it gives comments and suggestions and does not offer paraphrasing or rewriting. fastwork.co
