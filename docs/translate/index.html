Useful facts

  • Thailand joins the Apostille Convention on 28 February 2027. In member countries, one stamp (an apostille) then proves that a Thai public document is official, in place of legalisation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. mfa.go.th
  • An agency certifies a translation with a signed letter and its seal; no government body checks it. sawadeetranslations.com
  • For legalisation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the translator signs a line that certifies the translation and, since 10 February 2026, attaches a copy of their ID. consular.mfa.go.th
