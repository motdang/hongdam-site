What people ask

How much does a certified translation cost in Thailand?

A Bangkok agency lists certified Thai–English translation at ฿700, ฿800 or ฿950 a page, by tier. Standard translation starts at ฿1.5 a word. Government and embassy fees are charged on top. sawadeetranslations.com

What is a certified translation?

At an agency it is a translation with a signed statement that it is complete and accurate, carrying the company's seal. The agency's guide says this is not verification by any government body. sawadeetranslations.com

When does Thailand join the Apostille Convention?

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs says the accession takes full effect on 28 February 2027. Thailand deposited its instrument of accession on 30 June 2026, and an eight-month objection period runs before that date. mfa.go.th

Do visa and marriage papers need a translation?

An agency guide lists visa and immigration applications and marriage registration in Thailand among the papers that commonly need a certified translation. It adds that an authority sets its own specific requirements. sawadeetranslations.com
