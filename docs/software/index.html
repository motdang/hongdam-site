Team Wheel

A task board that looks like a wheel of big buttons. Each person sees their own work in their own language.

How it goes

  1. Write a job: what, why, and what done means.
  2. Give it to a person. It shows on their wheel, in their language.
  3. They press I'm on it, then Finished.
  4. You check it and close it.
For
Teams, clubs and charities that hand out jobs, in any of 16 languages.

Wholesale Desk

Products, wholesale price lists and invoices, for a business that sells to shops.

How it goes

  1. Add your products, each with its SKU (stock code) and price.
  2. Write an invoice. VAT is added when you switch it on, and a wholesale line under the minimum order is refused.
  3. Issue it. An issued invoice can no longer be changed.
  4. Add payments as they come in. Each save is logged with who made it.
For
A small trading business with a product list and wholesale buyers.
