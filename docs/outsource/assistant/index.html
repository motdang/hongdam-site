Useful facts

  • Thai labour law sets ordinary work at no more than 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week (Labour Protection Act, Section 23). mekongmigration.org
  • Overtime on a working day pays at least 1.5 times the hourly wage (Labour Protection Act, Section 61). mekongmigration.org
  • Under the Personal Data Protection Act (Section 40), a firm that handles personal data for a business acts on that business's instructions. The business prepares an agreement that controls what the firm does. cc.kmutt.ac.th
