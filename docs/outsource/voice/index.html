Good to know

  • Thai copyright law names a person who speaks or narrates a script as a performer (Section 4). How long does a Thai voice-over take, and how many edits come with it?

One Fastwork seller lists 1 day of work and 2 edits for its packages. Its work steps say 1 to 2 days after the price is agreed. fastwork.co

Does the speaker have rights in the recording?

The Thai Copyright Act gives a performer, including a person who narrates a script, exclusive rights to record the performance. It also covers copying a recording made without the performer's consent (Section 44). ipthailand.go.th
