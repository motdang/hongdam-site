Why 7-Eleven is loved here, and what กด 5 means at home

ทำไมใครๆ ก็รักเซเว่น และ กด 5 บนซองแปลว่าอะไรเมื่อเวฟที่บ้าน

Why does everyone here love 7-Eleven so much? And my rice box says กด 5 on the pack. What does that mean on the microwave at home?

ทำไมทุกคนที่นี่รักเซเว่นนัก แล้วบนซองข้าวกล่องเขียนว่า กด 5 ถ้าเวฟที่บ้านหมายความว่าอย่างไร

The love has numbers behind it, and the กด (kot, "press") on the pack is a button on the shop's own oven. CP All, the company that runs 7-Eleven in Thailand, reports 16,284 stores at the end of June 2026, 200 of them opened in that quarter 1. CP All runs 8,414 itself (about 52 percent); store business partners and sub-area stores run 7,870 (about 48 percent). Of all the stores, 86 percent stand alone and the rest sit in PTT gas stations 1.

Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai have 487, and 231 stay open all night

Mot Dang, the directory of Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai at motdang.net, lists 487 7-Elevens there. It knows 92 with a cash machine and 231 open all night, counted on 18 September 2026 2. 231 of 487 is just under half.

7-Elevens are sacred, with popsicles and beer

On 26 September I struck a line that called 7-Eleven interchangeable. My note: "That's not true, 7-Elevens are sacred," and more of the feeling: "hello welcome", popsicles and beer. The One Man Sideshow page carries the band "7-Elevens are sacred", with สวัสดีค่ะ (sawatdee kha, hello), เบียร์เย็น (bia yen, cold beer) and ไอติม (ai tim, popsicles) 2. The Wave It at Home page opens with สวัสดีค่ะ เซเว่นอีเลฟเว่น ยินดีต้อนรับค่ะ (sawatdee kha, seven-eleven, yindi tonrap kha: hello, welcome), and files popsicles and beer under "No wave. Open the fridge door and take one." 3

กด 5 is a button on the shop's oven

The number on a ready-meal pack is a program saved on the shop's microwave. Staff press one number and the oven sets its own power and time 3. Postjung, a Thai web board, described the oven on 23 April 2021 as industrial-grade stainless steel, over 30 kg, with keys numbered 1 to 0 in place of watts (W, the measure of an oven's power), and named Panasonic 4. Thai reports disagree on the model 3.

The pack names two store strengths, so one number runs for two times. 7-Eleven's online shop prints the Kurobuta pork curry rice like this: 1,800 W, press 6, 1:10; 1,300 W, press 6, 1:30; and for a home oven, 800 W, 1:45 to 2:00 5.

The number follows how cold the food is. Sipso rice soup is press 5 at room temperature and press 6 from the fridge, at 2 to 6 °C 6. Frozen meals start at 7 or 8 and add a second press 3. Two presses, like 6 and 2, add a short program after the first rather than doubling it: about 15 to 20 seconds in store 3.

The home times are a deduction

No table of each code's power and time has been seen published 3. The home times on the Wave It at Home page are its deduction, not figures from 7-Eleven. Watts times minutes is the heat the food takes in, and the store times give the heat for each code. A pack line that says "under 1,300 W" is read as 800 W, the middle of what the packs imply, about 650 to 1,050 W. Codes 2 to 6 rise by about ×1.46 per step. Code 1 is read off that curve with no pack seen. Codes 7 and 8 come from frozen packs. One check: Creator sea bass rice soup prints 800 W, 5:00 and 1,300 W, 3:00, which agree within 3 percent 3.

The page lists its gaps: the AB button appears on one pack, and code 1, and code 8 alone, have no pack with a printed time 3.

At 800 W, press 5 gives 1:30 to 2:00 on the page: it starts at 1:30 and adds 15 seconds at a time up to 2:00. Press 6 gives 1:45 to 2:40 3. A pack that prints its own home time stands against those ranges. The curry prints 1:45 to 2:00 for code 6 5. Sipso prints 1:00 to 1:30 at 800 W for code 5 6.

The watts sit on a sticker inside the oven door or on the back 3. A small home oven of 17 to 23 litres runs 700 to 900 W 7.

Press 4 is not 4 minutes

On Pantip, a Thai discussion forum, on 10 June 2021, a poster told by a shop clerk that กด 4 meant 4 minutes heated a Japanese sweet potato for 4 minutes and burned it. An account named น้องเปาเซเว่น (Nong Pao 7-Eleven) replied that a normal microwave takes about 1 to 1½ minutes, and that the pack has more 8.
