Say "gas" at a Thai pump and you are asking for LPG. Here is what to say

พูดว่า "แก๊ส" ที่ปั๊มไทย เท่ากับขอ LPG แล้วต้องพูดว่าอะไร

Dear Nan, I just rented a pickup. At the station I said "gas, please" and the attendant looked confused. Then he put a little sign with a red arrow on my hood. Which pump is diesel, and what is that sign? — Confused at the Pump in Chiang Rai

ถึงแนน เพิ่งเช่ารถกระบะมา ไปปั๊มแล้วบอกว่า "gas, please" เด็กปั๊มทำหน้างง แล้วก็วางป้ายที่มีลูกศรแดงไว้บนฝากระโปรงรถ หัวจ่ายไหนคือดีเซล แล้วป้ายนั้นคืออะไร — งงอยู่ที่ปั๊ม เชียงราย

Open Gas and Diesel

Nan Peacock, who runs Hongdam

Dear Confused,

The attendant heard a different fuel. In Thai, แก๊ส (gaet) means LPG or NGV. That is the gas some taxis and pickups run on. Petrol and diesel are both น้ำมัน (nam-man, fuel or oil), or they go by the name of the grade 1. Your pickup most likely wants ดีเซล (di-sen). The fuel flap or the owner's manual says which grades a vehicle takes 1.

The diesel pump says ดีเซล on the nozzle

Each nozzle carries a label with its fuel's name 1. The ones you meet most often:

Ethanol is an alcohol made from plants, mixed into the petrol. Octane is a number for how well a fuel resists burning too early in the engine. The names and uses come from the Mot Dang page 1. The prices are PTT Station's, in baht a litre, from 11 November 2026, read on 15 November 2. PTT's premium fuels go by "Super Power"; other station brands use their own names 1.

The arrow sign is a checklist for two people

The sign on your hood is magnetic. You name the fuel. The attendant slides the red arrow to that label. Then you both look at the label on the nozzle, and it matches the arrow 1. You see the arrow through the windshield, and the attendant sees it from the pump. A spoken word becomes a mark that two people can check. Toyota, the car maker, has a word for a guard like this: poka-yoke, "mistake-proofing" 1.

The US does it another way. There, most drivers fill their own tanks, so the nozzle does the guarding. A petrol nozzle is about 13/16 inch (21 mm) wide, and a diesel nozzle about 15/16 inch (24 mm) 4. A petrol car's filler pipe has a ring too narrow for the diesel nozzle 4. The other slip, petrol into a diesel car, fits 1.

What to say

A woman ends each line with ค่ะ (kha), a man with ครับ (khrap) 1.

The attendant may ask เติมอะไร (toem arai, which fuel?) or เต็มถังไหม (tem thang mai, full tank?) 1. In the car you pull the fuel-flap lever by your seat. The attendant takes cash, a card or a PromptPay QR scan at your window and brings the change and receipt 1.

On a scooter there is no sign

A scooter has no hood, so there is no arrow. The spoken grade and the nozzle's label do the work. The rental shop knows which grade the bike takes 1. A scooter tank takes about 4 to 8 litres 1. At the 11 November price of Gasohol 95, that is about ฿157 to ฿315 2. Out of town, village shops sell fuel in glass bottles, น้ำมันขวด (nam-man khuat) 1.

The names are changing

Thailand's energy planners name E20 and diesel B7 as the main fuels. Gasohol 91, Gasohol 95 and E85 (85% ethanol, for cars built to take it) are to be phased out, with 2027 as the target 3.

Diesel for the pickup, then, and a red arrow to agree on it.
