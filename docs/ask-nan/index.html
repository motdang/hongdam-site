    How do I stop guessing what to charge?

    จะตั้งราคาอย่างไรไม่ให้ต้องเดา

    Put the price on a ladder of round numbers and let orders move it one rung at a time: busy goes up, quiet goes down. Donald Shoup proposed this for parking, and Hongdam's prices run on it.
