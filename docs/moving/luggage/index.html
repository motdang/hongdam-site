Good to know

  • The State Railway of Thailand carries motorcycles by train. The fee starts at ฿560, and the bike reaches the destination station in 1 to 3 days. The sender brings the registration book or a copy and an ID card, and gives the recipient's name, phone, address and station. bangkokbiznews.com
  • The bike is wrapped in air bubble film, by the sender or with the staff's help. A bike not collected is kept at the station for up to 2 days. bangkokbiznews.com
  • Thailand Post announced EMS Jumbo in March 2023. It takes parcels of 30 to 200 kg to every province, islands included, in 2 to 4 days, with boxes under 200 cm on each side. Loss or damage pays up to ฿5,000 an item, and insurance up to ฿200,000 is optional. nationthailand.com
  • Thailand Post's March 2023 EMS Jumbo release lists motorcycles up to 400 cc among the items it carries, not big-bike shaped, and puts boxed items by weight from ฿200 for the first 30 kg. ryt9.com

What people ask

Can I send a motorbike by train in Thailand?

Yes. The State Railway of Thailand takes motorcycles at the parcel point of a station. The fee starts at ฿560, and the bike arrives in 1 to 3 days. bangkokbiznews.com

What papers does a motorbike need to go by train?

The registration book or a copy, the sender's ID card, and the recipient's name, phone, address and destination station. If someone else owns the bike, the owner's ID papers are needed. bangkokbiznews.com

Can Thailand Post send a motorbike?

Its EMS Jumbo service lists motorcycles up to 400 cc, not big-bike shaped, among the items it carries. Boxed items of 30 to 200 kg start at ฿200. ryt9.com

How heavy a parcel can a courier take?

Flash Express's Flash Bulky takes parcels of 5 to 100 kg, with fees from ฿50, according to a February 2022 report. thestandard.co

What happens if EMS Jumbo loses or breaks a parcel?

In its March 2023 announcement, Thailand Post set compensation at up to ฿5,000 an item for loss or damage. Insurance up to ฿200,000 is optional. nationthailand.com
