Good to know

  • People moving to Thailand import used household goods duty-free in reasonable amounts if owned and used before. suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th
  • The goods arrive no earlier than one month before and no later than six months after the owner. suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th
  • Duty-free appliances are one of each type per person, two per family. suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th
  • FIDI's Thailand customs guide, posted by Asian Tigers and updated 7 September 2024, lists duty-free entry for a foreign national with a one-year Non-Immigrant B visa and a one-year work permit, or a one-year Smart visa. It says holders of O, O-A, Elite and ED visas are not considered and pay duties. asiantigersgroup.com
  • The same guide gives clearance as 3 to 5 working days for a full container and 5 to 7 for a shared container or air freight, with the papers in order. The bill of lading must read Used Household Goods & Personal Effects; Thai Customs fines about ฿1,500 when the marks are missing or incomplete. asiantigersgroup.com

What people ask

How much does it cost to ship household goods to Thailand?

PSS Removals, a UK mover, lists a shared container from around £1,250 and a three-bed house at £5,500–5,700. Taxes and import duties are extra. pssremovals.com

Do I pay import duty on my household goods in Thailand?

Used household goods you owned and used abroad come in duty-free in reasonable amounts, if they arrive from one month before to six months after you. The airport's customs page lists a draft import declaration, your passport, proof that your residence changed, the bill of lading, a packing list and an application for duty exemption. suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th

How long does shipping household goods from the UK to Thailand take?

PSS Removals gives 6 to 8 weeks to the port for a full container and 8 to 12 weeks for a shared one. Customs clearance and delivery come after. pssremovals.com

What size container does a house need?

The Thaiger's relocation page says a 20-foot container suits a typical two- to three-bedroom home. A survey of the goods decides where a shared container stops costing less than a dedicated one. thethaiger.com

Can used household goods come by air?

FIDI's guide allows duty-free entry for one small air shipment and one sea shipment. The air waybill must read Used Personal Effects only. asiantigersgroup.com
