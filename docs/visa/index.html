Useful facts

  • Leaving Thailand without a re-entry permit ends the permission to stay. bangkok.immigration.go.th
  • The 90-day report (telling immigration where you live, every 90 days) is accepted from 15 days before to 7 days after the due date; a late report in person costs ฿2,000. immigration.go.th
  • The DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) is valid for five years, allows stays of up to 180 days each entry, and does not allow work for Thai companies or Thai clients. budapest.thaiembassy.org
