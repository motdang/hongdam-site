Useful facts

  • A Thai foundation registers at the district office of its principal office: the khet office in Bangkok, the amphoe office elsewhere. thailawonline.com
  • By the end of March each year, a foundation files an activity report, a balance sheet and an income and expenditure account certified by an authorised auditor, and the minutes of the board meetings held that year. thailawonline.com
  • A foundation keeps detailed financial records. It gets a tax ID within 60 days of registering or of starting business, whichever is later. thailawonline.com

What people ask

What does a Thai foundation file each year?

By the end of March it files an activity report, a balance sheet and an income and expenditure account certified by an authorised auditor, and the minutes of the board meetings held that year. It also keeps detailed financial records. thailawonline.com

Which gifts can a donor deduct from tax for 2569?

Thairath reports that an individual deducts only cash gifts, to recipients recognised under Thai law such as temples, state schools, state hospitals and certified charities, and that the gift must go through e-Donation. The total is capped at 10% of income after expenses and allowances. thairath.co.th
