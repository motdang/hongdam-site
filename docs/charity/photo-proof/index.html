Useful facts

  • North Carolina's Water Resources Development Grant Program asks grantees for geotagged digital photos as an alternative to a staff site visit, so the grant manager can check and overlay the photo locations in GIS software. deq.nc.gov
  • The same guide asks for at least two photos, in opposite directions, at every photo location, and gives 2.5 to 5 MB as the usual file size for a photo. deq.nc.gov
  • The guide says the coordinates in a smartphone photo may be less accurate than those from a GPS camera. A photo with no GPS section showing latitude and longitude in its file details is most likely not geotagged. deq.nc.gov
  • Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act defines personal data as any information relating to a person that enables identification, directly or indirectly (Section 6). cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • Before or when it collects personal data, the data controller tells the person the purpose, the data collected and how long it is kept, who may receive it, and the controller's contact details (Section 23). cc.kmutt.ac.th

What people ask

Can a photo show where and when it was taken?

A photo can carry its GPS coordinates when the phone's location setting is on. North Carolina's guide says most smartphones less than 5 years old can do this. On a computer, the file's Details tab shows a GPS section with latitude and longitude. deq.nc.gov

How exact is the place in a phone photo?

North Carolina's guide says the coordinates embedded in photos taken by smartphones may not be as accurate as those taken with a GPS-enabled camera. It does not give a distance. deq.nc.gov

Are photos of people personal data in Thailand?

The Personal Data Protection Act defines personal data as any information relating to a person that enables identification, directly or indirectly. It leaves out collection by a person for personal benefit or household activity only (Section 4). Before or when it collects data, a controller tells the person the purpose and how long it keeps the data (Section 23). cc.kmutt.ac.th
