What people ask

Can a new member of a LINE group see the old messages?

Not the chat messages from before the person joined. LINE's help page says a new member sees the Note and Album posts made while the invitation was pending, and no earlier chat messages. help.line.me

How many people fit in a LINE group?

Up to 500 members, the creator included. With "Members join automatically" on, up to 100. help.line.me

What happens to a group's history when someone leaves or the group is deleted?

A person who leaves can no longer see the past posts, albums and notes. LINE's help page says they stay for the members who remain. A deleted group loses everything posted in it, and it cannot be restored. help.line.me
