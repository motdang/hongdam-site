Good to know

  • A case for support, also called a case statement, holds the organisation's mission, vision and values. It answers who the organisation helps, what its track record is, what it plans and how it uses its money. learning.candid.org
  • Firebird reviews applications twice a year, with deadlines on 15 January and 15 June. A submission window opens two weeks before each deadline, and an application outside a window is not considered. firebirdresearchgrants.org
  • Firebird holds back ten percent of a grant until the final report and financial statement are received, and its funds are for direct research costs only. firebirdresearchgrants.org
  • EMKP takes applicants of any nationality and academic background, if they apply with a host institution that provides independent financial and ethical oversight. emkp.org
  • The Australian Embassy's Direct Aid Program takes applications only through its SmartyGrants portal, and applications submitted after the closing time are not considered. thailand.embassy.gov.au
  • The Revenue Department's e-Donation system takes in gifts to schools, places of worship, state hospitals and the public charities named in a Ministry of Finance notice. A receiving unit holds a 13-digit unit number and registers with its founding certificate, the signer's appointment and the signer's ID. epayapp.rd.go.th
  • IRS Publication 526 lists foreign organisations, other than certain Canadian, Israeli and Mexican charities, among those a US donor cannot deduct a gift to. A gift to a US charity for a programme run by a foreign charity is deductible when the gift is not earmarked for the foreign charity and the US charity approves the programme and keeps control of the funds. irs.gov

What people ask

What does grant writing mean?

Grant writing is preparing the application a funder asks for. The Sumitomo Foundation, for one, publishes an application guide, instructions and a form for its 2026 call, and takes applications in English or Japanese. sumitomo.or.jp

Are there grant writers for hire, and what do they charge?

Yes. A cost guide dated July 2026 puts a mid-level freelance grant writer at US$50–95 an hour and a foundation proposal at US$1,500–5,000. The guide's publisher sells AI grant software, and the page calls its figures industry ranges, not quotes. grantable.co

Where can I find a grant writer in Thailand?

Fastwork, a Thai freelance marketplace, lists consultants who help with funding proposals. One listing offers a one-hour online consultation for ฿1,000 and, for ฿5,500, up to 4 hours of consultation with a document of up to 15 pages. fastwork.co

How should an application be written to win a grant?

A funder's guidelines say what it checks. Firebird requires an itemised budget at current prices, caps indirect costs at 3%, and disqualifies an application whose letters of recommendation come inside the packet instead of directly from the recommender. firebirdresearchgrants.org

Where can I see examples of funded projects?

Some funders publish them. The Sumitomo Foundation lists the research projects it funded each year from 2010 to 2025, and posts its selection reviews for 2023, 2024 and 2025. sumitomo.or.jp

What is a case for support?

A case for support, also called a case statement, is the core document of a fundraising campaign, in Candid's words. It states the organisation's mission, vision and values, and answers who it helps, what its track record is, what it plans and how it uses its money. learning.candid.org

Is there a grant I can apply for at any time of year?

The Rufford Foundation's small grants for nature conservation have no deadlines, and applications go in at any time of year. Grants range from £7,000 to £18,000, and an unsuccessful applicant may apply again after 12 months. apply.ruffordsmallgrants.org

Which gifts count for the Thai deduction from 2026?

From 1 January 2026, gifts to temples, foundations, associations, funds and other bodies that give a deduction go through the Revenue Department's e-Donation system only. This holds for individuals and for companies and partnerships. thestandard.co
