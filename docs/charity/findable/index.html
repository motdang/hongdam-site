Useful facts

  • Google Images can show who made an image, how it may be used and its credit when the image carries licence metadata. The metadata goes in markup on the page or inside the file as IPTC photo metadata, which is added once per image. developers.google.com
  • Google calls alt text the most important attribute for image metadata. File names give it only light clues, and it also reads the captions and the text around an image. developers.google.com
  • A Wikidata item needs to meet one of three tests: a link to a page on Wikipedia or another Wikimedia project, a clearly identifiable entity that public sources describe, or a structural need in other items. wikidata.org
  • Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act allows personal data to be collected without consent to prepare historical documents or archives in the public interest, or for research or statistics, when suitable safeguards for the person's rights are in place and the Committee's notification is followed (Section 24). cc.kmutt.ac.th

What people ask

What does Omeka cost for putting a collection online?

Omeka.net hosts sites. Its page lists a free plan for one site with limited themes and plugins, then US$35, US$75, US$350 and US$1,000 a year for larger plans, taxes excluded. omeka.net

What is Omeka S?

Omeka S is open-source software for institutions that connect digital cultural heritage collections with other online resources. One installation runs many sites, and it publishes items as linked open data. Its page states no price. omeka.org

How do the credit and the licence stay with a photo?

They can sit inside the image file as IPTC photo metadata, so they travel with the file from page to page. Google's page says this is added once per image. For the Licensable badge, the licence field is required. developers.google.com

What does Google read to know what a photo shows?

Google's page names the alt text as the most important attribute, used along with computer vision and the content of the page. The file name gives light clues, and the captions and the text near the image add context. developers.google.com

Which things can have a Wikidata entry?

An item has to meet one of three tests on Wikidata's notability page. It has a link to a page on Wikipedia or another Wikimedia project. Or it is a clearly identifiable entity that can be described with serious, public references. Or it fills a structural need, such as making statements in other items more useful. wikidata.org
