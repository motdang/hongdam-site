Useful facts

  • A Google My Maps map starts with one layer and can have up to 10. Layers group features, for example one layer for restaurants and another for coffee shops. support.google.com
  • To embed a Google My Maps map on a website, the map must be public. The help page lists two sharing options: link sharing, which gives the URL to specific people, and public, which lets anyone find the map. support.google.com
  • uMap is free, open-source software for maps on OpenStreetMap layers, and the maps can be put on your own website. A community maintains it, and OpenStreetMap groups in Germany, Switzerland and France host copies. umap-project.org
  • OpenStreetMap's tile policy allows its map tiles on a website when the OpenStreetMap credit is shown clearly on the map. It limits heavy use, may block access without notice and offers best-effort availability. operations.osmfoundation.org
  • Google says Maps Embed API usage is available at no charge. It needs an API key and has no per-second or per-day limits. developers.google.com

What people ask

Is there a free tool to make a map of places?

uMap is free, open-source software. It builds maps on OpenStreetMap layers and lets you put them on your own website. umap-project.org

Can a Google map go on my website for free?

Google says Maps Embed API usage is available at no charge. It needs an API key, and it has no per-second or per-day limits. developers.google.com

How many layers can a Google My Maps map have?

A map is created with one layer, and it can have up to 10. Google's help page gives the example of one layer for restaurants and another for coffee shops. support.google.com

Can the places be exported from Google My Maps?

Yes. The help page says a map exports to KML or KMZ. Individual layers export to CSV, but whole maps do not. support.google.com

What does Felt cost, and is there a nonprofit discount?

Felt's free plan lets you make and annotate maps, share them publicly and get 10k map views a month. Team is US$199 and Business US$599 a month, billed yearly. US 501(c)(3) nonprofits get 30% off paid plans. felt.com
