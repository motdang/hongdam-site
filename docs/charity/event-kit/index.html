What people ask

What does a ticketing site charge for a charity event in Thailand?

Peatix charges organisers in Thailand 4.9% + ฿35 for each paid ticket, with payment-processing fees included. On a ฿500 ticket that is ฿59.50. An event with only free tickets pays no fee. help-organizer.peatix.com

Who pays the ticketing fee, the organiser or the guest?

On Ticketmelon the organiser picks one of three options: absorb all fees, pass all of them to ticket buyers, or split them. If the organiser absorbs them, buyers pay exactly the ticket price. A split moves in steps of 0.5%. organizer.ticketmelon.com

Is there a Thai website where a charity can raise money online?

Taejai.com (เทใจ) is a Thai giving community. Anyone with a project to improve society sends it in through an online form, and donors give to the projects. Its home page shows ฿614,462,630 given by 231,579 donors, with 1,413 projects funded. taejai.com

How much does catering cost per person for a charity event?

One caterer lists a buffet from ฿110 a person, a northern Thai khan tok set from ฿170 and a VIP set meal from ฿200. Boxed meals start at ฿60 a box. A Chinese-style table starts at ฿1,800, with tables and chairs included. wiangpingcatering.com

What does a hotel in Chiang Mai charge per person for a dinner or event?

Dusit Princess Chiang Mai lists ฿550 net per person for a half day with lunch and one coffee break, ฿650 for a full day with two coffee breaks and ฿700 for a cocktail night. It serves a buffet from 30 guests and a set menu to share for 10 to 29. The offer runs to 31 December 2026, and the hotel may change prices without notice. dusit.com

Which months have the fewest rainy days in Chiang Mai?

January has 0.7 rainy days on average, February 0.9, December 1.4 and March 2.3. August has 20.9. The table covers 1981 to 2015. chiangmai.net

Can a charity gala serve alcohol on a Buddhist holy day?

Alcohol sales stop on Buddhist holy days. Hotels and licensed entertainment venues are exempt. In 2026 the stop for Asalha Bucha and Buddhist Lent ran from 12.01 am on 29 July to midnight on 30 July. nationthailand.com

Can donors deduct a gift to a charity from their tax?

For tax year 2569 (2026), people deduct only cash gifts made through e-Donation, up to 10% of income. Gifts made another way do not count. thairath.co.th
