Useful facts

  • The Revenue Department's e-Donation system takes in gifts to schools, places of worship, state hospitals and the public charities named in a Ministry of Finance notice. A receiving unit holds a 13-digit unit number and registers with its founding certificate, the signer's appointment and the signer's ID. epayapp.rd.go.th
  • From 1 January 2026, a gift to a temple, foundation, association or fund that gives the donor a deduction goes through e-Donation only, according to the Revenue Department's notice. The donor needs no paper receipt. thestandard.co
  • Individual taxpayers may claim a double deduction for gifts made through e-Donation, within an overall limit of 10% of net assessable income. The extension covers gifts from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027 and awaits a Royal Decree, according to a Forvis Mazars note of 23 July 2026. forvismazars.com
  • A bank sends gift data to the Revenue Department for individual donors only, not for companies. The bank account is the receiving unit's own, opened by its signer and used for gifts only. After the donor agrees in the banking app, the record reaches e-Donation within two working days. epayapp.rd.go.th
  • IRS Publication 526 lists foreign organisations, other than certain Canadian, Israeli and Mexican charities, among those a US donor cannot deduct a gift to. A gift to a US charity for a programme run by a foreign charity is deductible when the gift is not earmarked for the foreign charity and the US charity approves the programme and keeps control of the funds. irs.gov
  • Wise pays baht out of a local Wise bank account in Thailand and uses the mid-market rate. Its Thailand page names Kasikornbank, Bangkok Bank and Siam Commercial Bank, with a limit per transfer. wise.com
  • Omise's restricted-business page lists donations among restricted financial services, and lists charitable fund-raising among the activities that TrueMoney Wallet, Alipay and WeChat Pay prohibit. It lists registration records for charities among the licences it asks for. omise.co

What people ask

What is e-Donation?

e-Donation is the Revenue Department's system for recording gifts to schools, places of worship, state hospitals, public charities and other units the law names. A donor who claims a deduction keeps no paper proof, and can read the record on the department's site at any hour. epayapp.rd.go.th

Does a gift through an e-Donation QR cost a fee?

The Revenue Department's Q&A says a gift through the QR code in the e-Donation scheme carries no fee. A QR sign that counts carries the words e-Donation. epayapp.rd.go.th

How does a donor claim the Thai deduction after a QR gift?

After scanning the unit's QR code and entering the amount, the banking app asks whether to send the gift data to the Revenue Department. If the donor agrees, the record is in e-Donation within two working days. The donor reads it on the department's site with the same login as online tax filing. epayapp.rd.go.th

Which gifts count for the Thai deduction from 2026?

From 1 January 2026, gifts to temples, foundations, associations, funds and other bodies that give a deduction go through the Revenue Department's e-Donation system only. This holds for individuals and for companies and partnerships. thestandard.co

Can a donor in the US deduct a gift to a Thai charity?

IRS Publication 526 does not allow a deduction for a gift to a foreign organisation, other than certain Canadian, Israeli and Mexican charities. A gift to a US charity for a programme run by a foreign charity is deductible when it is not earmarked for the foreign charity and the US charity approves the programme and keeps control of the funds. irs.gov

What does PayPal take when a charity in Thailand gets a gift?

On PayPal Thailand's business fee page, a payment from a sender in Thailand costs 3.90% plus a fixed fee, and from a sender outside Thailand 4.40% plus a fixed fee. The fixed fee is ฿11 on baht, US$0.30 on dollars and £0.20 on pounds. The page lists no separate charity rate. paypal.com

What does a bank transfer from abroad cost the receiving charity?

Kasikornbank's fee announcement, in force from 1 October 2026, sets 0.25% of an incoming international transfer, with a minimum of ฿200 and a maximum of ฿500. On a gift under ฿80,000 the minimum of ฿200 applies. kasikornbank.com

What do online fundraising platforms take?

Taejai, a Thai platform, takes a 10% service fee. Its FAQ says the fee covers the website, online payment costs and the staff who screen and follow projects. Taejai adds the fee on top: a project that needs ฿10,000 shows ฿11,000. linehoro.taejai.com
