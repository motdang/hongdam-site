Useful facts

  • Before or when it collects personal data, the data controller tells the person the purpose, the data collected and how long it is kept, who may receive it, and the controller's contact details (Section 23). cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • For a child under ten, consent to the use of personal data comes from the holder of parental responsibility. For an older minor, the parent's consent is added when the act is not one the minor may do alone (Section 20). cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • Health data, disability, religious beliefs, criminal records and biometric data are among the data that may not be collected without explicit consent from the person, apart from the exceptions the Act lists (Section 26). cc.kmutt.ac.th
  • The Act sets an administrative fine of up to ฿1,000,000 for a data controller that does not give the Section 23 notice (Section 82). cc.kmutt.ac.th

What must a sign-up form tell volunteers about their data?

Section 23 of Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act lists the purpose, the data collected and how long it is kept, who may receive it, the controller's contact details and the person's rights. The notice comes before or at the time of collection. cc.kmutt.ac.th

Can a form ask about health or religion?

Section 26 bars collecting health data and religious beliefs without explicit consent from the person. It lists exceptions, among them preventing harm to a person's life, body or health and the lawful activities of a foundation, association or other non-profit body with a political, religious, philosophical or trade union purpose, for its members and regular contacts, when it does not pass the data outside the body. cc.kmutt.ac.th
