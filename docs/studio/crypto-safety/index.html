How bad can it get

Each step is worse than the one before. Plan for the last one.

  1. 1

    Paused for weeks

    Your coins show on the screen, but you cannot move or sell them while the price falls.

  2. 2

    Paid back in part, years later

    Mt. Gox failed in 2014. Payments began in July 2024, and some customers are still waiting now. source · decrypt.co

  3. 3

    Other exchanges slow down

    Many people take money out at once. Exchanges, Thai ones too, slow or stop withdrawals. Zipmex, with many Thai users, stopped them in July 2022. source · reuters.com

  4. 4

    USDT stops being one dollar

    This week Tether froze US$1.5 billion of USDT. One company can stop any USDT address.

  5. 5

    Your bank account is frozen

    Sell crypto to a stranger and the baht may be stolen money. Thai banks freeze the account that receives it. source · keptbykrungsri.com

  6. 6

    Fake callers

    People pretend to be Binance or the police, or offer to get your coins back for a fee.

  7. 7

    Your own phone or laptop

    One laptop may have opened Binance. A photo of your recovery words or a reused password can empty your wallet, and nobody can pause it.

  8. 8

    Your family cannot reach it

    You are in hospital. Does anyone in your family know where your coins are, and who might pretend to help?
