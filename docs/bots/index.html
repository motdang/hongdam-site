Priceราคา

ราคาขึ้นลงตามยอดซื้อ ทุกวันจันทร์ ถ้าสัปดาห์ก่อนขายเกิน 80% ของคิว ราคาขึ้นหนึ่งขั้น ต่ำกว่า 60% ลงหนึ่งขั้น ระหว่างนั้นคงเดิมPurchases set the price. Each Monday we look at the week before. If more than 80% of the slots were sold, the price goes up one step. If fewer than 60% were sold, it goes down one step. In between, it stays. Each product's price stays within three steps of its starting price. The share changes the same way, in percent.

It is the parking-price rule of Donald Shoup, from his book The High Cost of Free Parking, as San Francisco uses it. The price the page shows is the price you pay. Each week's changes are listed at /api/bots.
