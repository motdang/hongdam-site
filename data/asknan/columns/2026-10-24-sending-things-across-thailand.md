Dear Stuck,

The scooter does not have to ride all the way. It can take the train to Bangkok, for ฿960 in fees plus a freight charge set at the station [9]. A 20 kg suitcase goes by courier or Thailand Post for ฿200 to ฿320. These prices come from the delivery companies' own pages, checked on 20 October 2026. At the counter, staff weigh and measure the item, and that sets the final price [1].

## A suitcase has four ways to go

| Way | 20 kg to Bangkok | What the company says |
|---|---|---|
| Flash Express, Flash Bulky | ฿200 | ฿10 a kg, plus ฿3 fuel. Free pickup at your door, booked in the app, or take it to a branch. A passport is accepted as ID. [1][2] |
| Nim Express | ฿205 | Rate sheet from 22 January 2025, for items up to 155 cm. The price uses the weight or the volume weight, whichever is more. Volume weight is length × width × height in cm ÷ 6,000. [3][4] |
| Thailand Post EMS (express mail) | ฿320 | Arrives in 1 working day when posted before 16:00. The three sides add up to 120 cm at most. The longest side is 60 cm at most. Up to 240 cm in total is accepted if you tell the post office first. [5][6] |
| BKS bus parcel | box D from ฿105 | Up to 20 kg and 200 cm, on a 2024 list. That list has no Chiang Mai to Bangkok price. BKS (บขส., the government bus company) says a parcel arrives within one day. [7] |

The BKS parcel office is at the Arcade bus station in Chiang Mai. In Bangkok, the receiver shows ID at the counter [1]. A bag can also fly. Thai AirAsia's fee sheet for flights inside Thailand lists ฿663 for a 20 kg checked bag [8].

## The scooter takes the train, 1 to 3 days

The State Railway of Thailand (SRT) carries motorbikes from every station except those on the eastern line [9]. Its fee table covers bikes up to 125 cc engine size, going over 600 km. It lists ฿800, plus ฿100 to load and ฿60 to unload: ฿960 in all. The table applies from 1 October 2022. Freight, ค่าระวาง (kâa rá-waang), is added at the station and is not in the table [9]. Chiang Mai to Bangkok is over 600 km. The Northern Line is 751 km long [10].

At the station's parcel counter, the sender hands in:

- the green registration book, เล่มเขียว (lêm kǐao), original or copy;
- a Thai ID card;
- the receiver's name, phone, address and station.

The SRT notice does not mention a passport. The bike is wrapped in bubble wrap, by the sender or by the staff. The receiver collects it within 2 days with the receipt, and a photo of the receipt is accepted. The SRT customer line is 1690, open 24 hours [9].

Thailand Post's Logispost takes bikes at some post offices. Its call centre, 1545, can say which ones [5][11]. A bike up to 150 cc costs ฿1,200 to ฿2,640, by zone, on a 2024 list. Chiang Mai is zone 6 and Bangkok is zone 1. Wrapping costs ฿160 more, and delivery takes 5 to 7 working days. The page does not give the exact fare between those two zones [11].

## Two kinds of tracking number

A Thailand Post number has two letters, eight digits, a check digit and TH, like EF123456785TH. A check digit is a last number that checks the others. This pattern is the Universal Postal Union's S10 format [12]. That number goes into track.thailandpost.co.th [13]. A number from another delivery company goes into 17TRACK, which says it covers more than 3,500 companies [14]. It also works on that company's own page. Send It links Flash, KEX, J&T, Nim Express and SPX [1]. For a scooter on the train, the SRT notice gives the 1690 phone line and names no number to track [9].
